2. **"Liptior" corrected to "Lipitor"** (Pfizer atorvastatin) everywhere, including L1.5 Q3.
3. **ACE-cough counseling script (L1.2):** the source told patients the doctor could switch them to "another drug from the same group (like Tareg)". ARBs are a different class; the script now says "a related group (ARB such as losartan or valsartan)" and adds the angioedema warning.

8. **Lesson 4.8, Q9.** The original question was "Next course: GI/Respiratory". It was course-navigation trivia, not a test of learning. It was replaced with an integration question on the most dangerous sedative combination (opioid + benzodiazepine + pregabalin + alcohol).
