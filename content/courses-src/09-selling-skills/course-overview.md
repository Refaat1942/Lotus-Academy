## Course description

This course is for pharmacists, pharmacy technicians and counter staff working in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt. It teaches ethical, consultative selling: the kind of selling that starts from the patient's need, respects the limits of over-the-counter advice, and builds a customer who comes back and recommends the pharmacy to the family. It deliberately rejects pressure tactics. In a pharmacy, the best sale is the right product, for the right person, at a price they can sustain.

The course follows the real journey of a customer at the counter. You will start with the professional mindset and the boundaries of OTC advice, then learn how to greet and discover needs, recommend with clear Features-Advantages-Benefits language, build a helpful basket with genuine add-ons, handle objections such as price, inflation and "someone recommended another product", and finally close respectfully, retain the customer and follow up. Every lesson includes ready-to-use scripts in English and Arabic, checklists, common mistakes and scenarios from busy Egyptian branches, including WhatsApp and phone orders.

Each lesson takes about 80 minutes and ends with a 10-question quiz. A 20-question final exam covers the whole course. Pharmacists counsel and refer; they do not diagnose. Always follow the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority and the Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the difference between consultative selling and pushing products, and apply the patient-first principle in daily decisions
2. Recognise when not to sell and when to refer a customer to a doctor, using clear red-flag criteria
3. Run a structured greeting and needs-discovery conversation using open and closed questions and active listening
4. Recommend products using Features-Advantages-Benefits, and explain generics versus brands honestly
5. Suggest complementary products and bundles only when they genuinely help the customer
6. Handle price, trust and comparison objections with respect, using concrete model dialogues
7. Close a sale respectfully, run loyalty and follow-up routines, and read simple KPIs without encouraging unethical targets
