## Course description

This course is for pharmacists, pharmacy technicians, cashiers and branch managers working in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt. A pharmacy is a healthcare place that also runs as a retail business, and both sides depend on the same daily discipline: an orderly store, reliable stock, correct storage, accurate prices and cash, and records that stand up to any inspection.

The course turns these topics into practical routines you can apply on your next shift. You will learn how to organise zones and shelves, apply FEFO and receiving checks, protect refrigerated products and respond when a fridge fails, keep prices, promotions and cash accurate, reduce losses, and build the habits that keep a branch inspection-ready every day. Prescriptions and restricted medicines are covered only in general principles: the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority / Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP always come first and must be checked.

Each lesson includes checklists, tables, worked examples and an Egyptian-pharmacy scenario, followed by a ten-question quiz. The course ends with a twenty-question final exam. A short video script accompanies every lesson so teams can see the routines in practice.

## Learning outcomes

1. Arrange store zones, shelves and displays so customers can choose safely and quickly
2. Apply FEFO, receiving checks and expiry monitoring to keep stock safe and saleable
3. Order, count and reconcile stock and handle shortages and substitutions correctly
4. Store products at the right conditions and keep accurate temperature records
5. Respond to a temperature excursion or fridge failure with safe general principles and escalate to the pharmacist and manager
6. Keep prices, promotions, receipts and cash accurate, and apply simple loss-prevention habits
7. Maintain hygiene, keep honest records and follow current rules and the Lotus SOP for prescriptions, restricted medicines and inspection readiness
