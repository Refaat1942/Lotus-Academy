## 1. How antifungals work

- **Azoles** (fluconazole, itraconazole, ketoconazole, clotrimazole, miconazole) inhibit ergosterol synthesis, a key part of the fungal cell membrane. Systemic azoles also inhibit human CYP450 enzymes, which explains their interactions
- **Polyenes** (amphotericin B, nystatin) bind ergosterol and make holes in the fungal membrane
- **Allylamines** (terbinafine) block an earlier step of ergosterol synthesis and are very effective against dermatophytes

## 2. Systemic antifungals

| Generic | Brand (Egypt) | Manufacturer | Spectrum | Usual dosing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fluconazole** | Diflucan, Flucoral | Pfizer / Pharco, Memphis | Candida, cryptococcus | 150mg single dose (vaginal) or 200–400mg OD as prescribed |
| **Itraconazole** | Sporanox | Janssen / generic | Dermatophytes, aspergillus | 200mg OD–BD |
| **Ketoconazole** | Nizoral (oral use limited) | Janssen | Candida, dermatophytes | 200mg OD — oral use declining because of hepatotoxicity |
| **Voriconazole** | Vfend | Pfizer (hospital) | Invasive aspergillosis | IV/oral, specialist |
| **Amphotericin B** | Fungizone | Hospital | Severe systemic infections | IV — nephrotoxic |
| **Terbinafine** | Lamisil | Novartis / generic | Dermatophytes, onychomycosis | 250mg OD — about 6 weeks for fingernails, 12 weeks for toenails, as prescribed |

**Fluconazole interactions:** raises statin levels (myopathy risk); raises the warfarin INR; adds to QT prolongation with amiodarone and other QT-prolonging drugs. Oral fluconazole is avoided in pregnancy — use topical treatment and refer.

**Terbinafine tablets:** rarely cause liver injury — counsel to report dark urine, yellow eyes or persistent nausea; taste disturbance can occur.

## 3. Topical antifungals — Egyptian market

| Generic | Brand | Form | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clotrimazole | Canesten, Clotrim | Cream, vaginal tablet (pessary) | Candida, tinea |
| Miconazole | Daktarin | Cream, powder, oral gel | Skin infections; oral thrush gel |
| Terbinafine | Lamisil | Cream, tablets | Dermatophytes, onychomycosis |
| Nystatin | Nystatin oral suspension | Suspension | Oral candidiasis, including infants |
| Econazole | Ecoderm | Cream | Tinea, candida |

**Important:** miconazole oral gel is absorbed enough to raise the INR markedly in patients on warfarin — avoid the combination unless the prescriber agrees and monitors.

### 3.1 Topical or systemic?

| Situation | Usual choice |
|---|---|
| Athlete's foot, ringworm, groin tinea limited in area | Topical azole or terbinafine cream |
| Widespread tinea, scalp ringworm, nail infection | Systemic treatment — refer to doctor or dermatologist |
| Uncomplicated vaginal candidiasis | Clotrimazole pessary/cream or a single fluconazole 150mg dose (as prescribed) |
| Oral thrush in an infant | Nystatin suspension (miconazole gel only per prescriber in young infants) |

**Refer:** pregnancy, first episode of vaginal symptoms, unusual discharge or smell, fever, diabetes or immunosuppression, nail infection, scalp infection in children, no improvement after 2 weeks of correct topical use.

## 4. Counselling

**Counselling script (Arabic):**

> "كريم الفطريات يتدهن مرتين يومياً لمدة 2–4 أسابيع حتى بعد اختفاء الأعراض بأسبوع. ديفلوكان للفطريات المهبلية قرص واحد فقط. لو عندك أدوية للقلب أو الضغط، قول للدكتور قبل ديفلوكان."

In English: apply the antifungal cream twice daily for 2–4 weeks, continuing for one week after the symptoms disappear. Diflucan for vaginal thrush is a single tablet only. If you take heart or blood pressure medicines, tell the doctor before Diflucan.

Also counsel: keep the area clean and dry, dry between the toes, avoid sharing towels, and wear cotton underwear.

## 5. Scenario

> A woman is self-treating recurrent vaginal candidiasis and asks to buy Diflucan 150mg every week without a prescription.

**Model response:** recurrent candidiasis (4 or more episodes a year) needs a physician workup to look for causes such as diabetes or immunosuppression and to confirm the diagnosis. It is not suitable for repeated self-treatment. Ask about pregnancy and other medicines. Advise a proper topical alternative for a mild current episode if appropriate, and refer.

Pharmacist: "Because it keeps coming back, it is important to see a gynaecologist to find the reason and get the right plan. For now, this cream can relieve you, but please book the visit this week."

## Key takeaways

- Diflucan (fluconazole) — a single 150mg dose is standard for uncomplicated vaginal candidiasis
- Topical Canesten and Daktarin are first line for limited skin tinea in Egypt
- Fluconazole and miconazole oral gel raise the warfarin INR — a common Egyptian interaction
- Oral ketoconazole is declining because of hepatotoxicity
- Terbinafine (Lamisil) is preferred for onychomycosis
- Pharco and Memphis fluconazole generics are affordable options
- Recurrent candidiasis needs a physician workup, not repeated self-treatment
