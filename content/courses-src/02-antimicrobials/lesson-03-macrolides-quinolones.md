## 1. Macrolides

### 1.1 Mechanism & evidence

Macrolides bind the 50S ribosomal subunit and inhibit bacterial protein synthesis. Azithromycin reaches high tissue concentrations and has a long half-life, which allows short courses. Azithromycin and clarithromycin are WHO AWaRe Watch antibiotics.

| Generic | Brand (Egypt) | Manufacturer | Usual adult dosing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azithromycin** | Zithromax, Azithrocin | Pfizer / Pharco, Memphis | 500mg OD × 3 days |
| **Clarithromycin** | Klacid, Clabact | Abbott / generic | 500mg BD × 7 days (or as prescribed) |
| **Erythromycin** | Erythrocin | Limited | 250–500mg QDS |

**Indications:** atypical pneumonia, streptococcal pharyngitis in penicillin-allergic patients, and H. pylori triple therapy (clarithromycin with a PPI and amoxicillin).

**Interactions:**

- Clarithromycin and erythromycin strongly inhibit CYP3A4: they raise simvastatin and atorvastatin levels → myopathy risk. Azithromycin has much less effect on statins
- Macrolides can raise the warfarin INR — advise extra INR monitoring as the prescriber directs
- QT prolongation, especially with amiodarone (Cordarone) and other QT-prolonging drugs

## 2. Fluoroquinolones (AWaRe WATCH)

| Generic | Brand (Egypt) | Usual adult dosing | Key points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ciprofloxacin** | Ciprobay, Ciprodar | 500mg BD | Tendon rupture, QT, CNS effects |
| **Levofloxacin** | Tavanic, Levoxin | 500mg OD | Respiratory infections |
| **Moxifloxacin** | Avalox | 400mg OD | QT prolongation |

**Egyptian resistance:** fluoroquinolone resistance in E. coli is high in Egyptian surveys (often reported above 50% in hospital studies). Avoid empiric ciprofloxacin for uncomplicated cystitis when an Access option is suitable.

| Avoid / caution | Interaction |
|---|---|
| Children and adolescents under 18 — generally avoided (cartilage risk) unless a specialist decides otherwise | Antacids, calcium, iron and zinc reduce absorption — take ciprofloxacin at least 2 hours before or 6 hours after them |
| Pregnancy and breastfeeding | Corticosteroids increase tendon rupture risk |
| Previous fluoroquinolone tendon disorder | Warfarin — INR may rise |
| Older patients (over 60) and kidney disease — higher tendon risk | Other QT-prolonging drugs |

## 3. Tetracyclines

| Generic | Brand (Egypt) | Usual dosing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Doxycycline** | Vibramycin, Doxymycin | 100mg BD (or as prescribed) | Acne, atypical infections, malaria prophylaxis |
| **Minocycline** | Minocin (limited) | 100mg BD | Acne |
| **Tetracycline** | Generic | 250–500mg QDS | Cheaper; more GI upset |

Doxycycline counselling: swallow with a full glass of water while sitting or standing and do not lie down for at least 30 minutes (oesophageal irritation); avoid taking at the same time as milk, antacids or iron; use sun protection. Tetracyclines are avoided in pregnancy and in children under 8 years (tooth staining).

**Counselling script (Arabic):**

> "سيبروباي بنستخدمه بحذر في مصر لأن بكتيريا البول مقاومة ليه كتير. لو حسيت ألم في أوتار الركبة أو الكاحل، وقف الدواء وروح للدكتور. الدوكسيسيكلين ممنوع في الحمل والأطفال الصغيرين، ويسبب حساسية شمس — استخدم واقي."

In English: we use Ciprobay carefully in Egypt because urinary bacteria are often resistant to it. If you feel pain in the tendons of the knee or ankle, stop and see the doctor. Doxycycline is not used in pregnancy or young children and causes sun sensitivity — use sunscreen.

## 4. Pharmacy scenario

### Scenario 2.3 — Fluoroquinolone for UTI

> Prescription: Ciprobay 500mg BD × 7 days for uncomplicated cystitis in a healthy 28-year-old woman.

**Model response:**

1. Stewardship concern — an Access option such as nitrofurantoin or cephalexin may be preferred when susceptibility is unknown; discuss with the prescriber politely, do not change the prescription yourself
2. Confirm she is not pregnant or breastfeeding — fluoroquinolones are avoided
3. If dispensed: counsel on tendon pain, hydration, and separating doses from antacids, calcium and iron
4. Warn: stop and contact the physician if joint or tendon pain, palpitations or numbness occur
5. Refer if fever, flank pain or blood in urine — possible kidney infection

Pharmacist: "Doctor, I have a patient with a Ciprobay prescription for simple cystitis. Resistance of E. coli to ciprofloxacin is high in our area; would you consider an alternative such as nitrofurantoin or cephalexin?"

## Key takeaways

- Zithromax 3-day course is widely used in Egypt for respiratory infections; it is a Watch antibiotic
- Ciprobay is AWaRe Watch; high E. coli resistance limits empiric use for UTI
- Fluoroquinolones are generally avoided in children under 18 and in pregnancy
- Doxycycline: photosensitivity, take upright with water, and avoid in pregnancy and children under 8
- Clarithromycin + statin — monitor for myopathy; azithromycin has less interaction
- Klacid is a component of H. pylori eradication regimens in Egypt
