## 1. Aminoglycosides

| Drug | Brand (Egypt) | Route | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gentamicin** | Gentamycin | IV/IM | Trough levels, serum creatinine |
| **Amikacin** | Amikin | IV/IM | Same — broader spectrum |
| **Tobramycin** | Tobramycin | IV/inhaled | Cystic fibrosis, Pseudomonas |

**Mechanism:** irreversible binding to the 30S ribosomal subunit → bactericidal. Killing is concentration-dependent, which is why once-daily dosing works well.

**Toxicity:** nephrotoxicity (usually reversible) and ototoxicity — hearing loss or balance problems (often irreversible). Once-daily dosing and trough-level monitoring reduce toxicity. In community pharmacy you will mostly meet aminoglycosides as injections prescribed after a hospital or clinic visit, and as eye or ear drops.

**Cautions:** myasthenia gravis (relative contraindication). Avoid combining with furosemide, vancomycin and other nephrotoxic or ototoxic drugs unless the prescriber is monitoring closely. Ask the patient to report ringing in the ears, dizziness or reduced urine output.

## 2. Metronidazole — Egyptian market

| Brand | Manufacturer | Strengths |
|---|---|---|
| **Flagyl** | Sanofi / EIPICO generic | 250mg, 500mg tablets; suspension |
| **Metronidazole** | Pharco, Memphis | Generic tablets |

Metronidazole covers anaerobic bacteria and protozoa.

| Indication | Usual adult dosing (follow the prescription) |
|---|---|
| Anaerobic infections (for example dental) | 400–500mg TDS × 7–10 days |
| C. difficile (non-severe) | 400–500mg TDS × 10–14 days — current guidelines prefer oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin; metronidazole is used when these are not available |
| Giardia / amoebiasis | 400mg TDS × 5–7 days |
| Trichomonas | 2g single dose or 400mg BD × 7 days (partner treatment needed) |
| Bacterial vaginosis | 400mg BD × 7 days |

**Critical interaction:** alcohol → disulfiram-like reaction (nausea, vomiting, flushing, headache, fast heartbeat). Avoid alcohol during treatment AND for 48 hours after the last dose. Metronidazole also increases the effect of warfarin (raised INR). Common side effects are a metallic taste and nausea; long courses can cause peripheral neuropathy.

**Counselling script (Arabic):**

> "فلاجيل ممنوع مع الكحول خالص — ولا حتى جرعة صغيرة — وكمان لمدة يومين بعد آخر قرص. لو حسيت تنميل في الرجلين أو اليدين، كلم الدكتور."

In English: no alcohol at all with Flagyl — not even a small amount — and for two days after the last tablet. If you feel numbness in your feet or hands, call the doctor.

## 3. Antiparasitics — relevance in Egypt

| Drug | Brand | Indication | Usual dosing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Albendazole** | Zentel, Andazol | Ascariasis, hookworm; neurocysticercosis (specialist) | 400mg single dose for common worms; longer courses only as prescribed |
| **Mebendazole** | Vermox | Pinworm, roundworm | Pinworm: 100mg single dose, repeated after 2 weeks; roundworm/hookworm: 100mg BD × 3 days |
| **Praziquantel** | Biltricide | **Schistosomiasis** (bilharzia, historically endemic in the Nile Delta) | 40mg/kg as a single day's treatment (may be split into 2 doses), as prescribed |
| **Tinidazole** | Fasigyn | Giardia, amoebiasis | 2g single dose |

**Schistosomiasis:** a long-standing public health issue in rural Egypt, addressed through praziquantel mass treatment campaigns. Refer a patient with blood in the urine or stool, or a history of canal water contact with symptoms, for medical assessment.

**Cautions:** albendazole and mebendazole are generally avoided in pregnancy (especially the first trimester) — refer. For children under 2 years, follow the prescriber's advice. With pinworm, treat the whole household and counsel on hygiene: short nails, hand washing, washing bedding.

## 4. Antinal & nifuroxazide

| Product | Active | Egyptian practice issue |
|---|---|---|
| **Antinal** | Nifuroxazide | Sold OTC for paediatric diarrhoea — NOT a substitute for ORS |
| **Normix** | Rifaximin | Prescription use: traveller's diarrhoea, hepatic encephalopathy |

**Correct paediatric diarrhoea management:** ORS + zinc, continued feeding or breastfeeding, ± probiotics. Antibiotics only for dysentery (bloody stool) or suspected cholera, as decided by a doctor according to guidelines. Refer urgently: signs of dehydration (sunken eyes, no tears, little urine, drowsiness), blood in stool, high fever, persistent vomiting, or an infant under 6 months.

## 5. Pharmacy scenario

### Scenario 2.4 — Flagyl and alcohol

> A patient on Flagyl 500mg TDS for a dental infection asks whether "just one glass" of wine at a wedding is safe.

**Model response:**

1. Clear prohibition — risk of a disulfiram-like reaction
2. Explain the symptoms: severe nausea, vomiting, flushing, fast heartbeat
3. No alcohol during the course and for 48 hours after the last dose
4. Complete the full antibiotic course — do not skip doses to drink
5. Report tingling or numbness (peripheral neuropathy)

Pharmacist: "Even one glass can make you very sick with this medicine. Please avoid alcohol completely until two full days after your last tablet. Keep taking the course on time."

## Key takeaways

- Gentamicin is mainly hospital use; TDM is essential; ototoxicity can be irreversible
- Flagyl is the most recognised metronidazole brand; generics are available
- NO alcohol with metronidazole during the course and for 48 hours after
- Biltricide (praziquantel) treats schistosomiasis, a long-standing problem in rural Egypt
- Antinal OTC misuse — ORS and zinc are first line for paediatric diarrhoea
- Zentel (albendazole) is a common anthelmintic in Egypt; avoid in pregnancy
