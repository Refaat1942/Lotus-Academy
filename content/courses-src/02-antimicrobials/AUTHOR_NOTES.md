## Identity kept
- Course ID EG-MED-02, lesson IDs EG-MED-02-L01 to L08, and all lesson file names are the same as the legacy course. Overview H1 titles, duration (14 hours), pass mark (70% per lesson quiz) and prerequisites are kept; Language row added.

## Quiz answer corrections
- L07 Q2 (Tamiflu start window): the legacy key marked "2 weeks" as correct. Corrected to "within 48 hours of symptom onset".
- L02 Q5 asked which company makes Velosef (key: EIPICO). Velosef is cefradine, not cefadroxil, and the manufacturer could not be confirmed, so the question now asks what Velosef is (1st-generation cephalosporin, cefradine).
- L08 Q9 ("Course 2 next topic: diabetes") was trivia; replaced with an application question on referral after 48–72 hours without improvement.
- Lessons 6, 7 and 8 had questions with only 1–3 options; all now have 4 options with plausible distractors. Topics and correct answers kept.

## Clinical content corrections
- L01 AWaRe table: clindamycin was listed under Watch; WHO classifies it as Access. Moved to Access, and clarithromycin added to Watch.
- L01: "Rx retention 2 years minimum per Egyptian pharmacy law" replaced with "for the period required by current EDA / Ministry of Health rules and the Lotus SOP" (we cannot verify the exact legal period).
- L01: "Nifuroxazide OTC misuse contributes to resistance" reworded. The main concern is OTC misuse instead of ORS. Whether nifuroxazide itself drives resistance is not well established.
- L02: Velosef was listed as cefadroxil (EIPICO). Corrected to cefradine (cephradine); manufacturer given as "local manufacture". Cefadroxil kept as Duricef.
- L02: penicillin-allergy table made more conservative. After anaphylaxis, no beta-lactam is dispensed without the prescriber's explicit confirmation. A mild rash should still be confirmed with the prescriber.
- L02: the odd row "Augmentin companion — often co-prescribed" became a note telling pharmacists to query duplicate beta-lactam therapy with the prescriber.
- L03: "FQs contraindicated in children <18" softened to "generally avoided unless a specialist decides" (they are used in some paediatric indications). Antacid separation corrected from "2h" to "2 hours before or 6 hours after" for ciprofloxacin. Clarified that the statin interaction is mainly with clarithromycin/erythromycin, not azithromycin. Added older age as a tendon-rupture risk factor and doxycycline administration advice.
- L03 scenario: the pharmacist now discusses alternatives with the prescriber instead of implying a substitution.
- L04: mebendazole dosing for pinworm corrected (100mg single dose repeated after 2 weeks; 100mg BD × 3 days is for roundworm/hookworm). Added that current guidelines prefer oral vancomycin/fidaxomicin over metronidazole for C. difficile. Added pregnancy caution for albendazole/mebendazole and dehydration red flags.
- L06: added the miconazole oral gel–warfarin interaction, avoiding oral fluconazole in pregnancy, terbinafine tablet duration and liver warning, and referral criteria. Distractor "Liptior" corrected to "Lipitor".
- L07: acyclovir zoster dose corrected from "800mg TDS" to "800mg five times daily × 7 days". Duplicate Valtrex row merged. Dexamethasone limited to severe COVID-19 needing oxygen. In the Paxlovid scenario the pharmacist now agrees the statin pause with the prescriber and checks renal function.
- L08: the case brand "Liptior" corrected to Lipitor (atorvastatin). The quick-reference row "Penicillins: Augmentin, Amoxil, Ceporex" was split, because Ceporex is a cephalosporin. Added a reporting-pathways section to cover learning objective 4.

## Formatting changes
- Code blocks, emojis and "Next lesson" links removed (format rules). Key takeaways converted to bullet lists. The references are kept as plain text before the quiz.
- Legacy EGP price columns were removed: the format forbids unverified prices and Egyptian prices have changed a lot. Lessons now tell staff to check the current price in the pharmacy system.

- Brand ↔ manufacturer pairings from the legacy content (for example Ceporex/Memphis generic, Clavimox/Amoun-Memphis, Hyzide, Azithrocin, Clabact, Levoxin, Doxymycin, Flucoral, Andazol) were kept but not independently verified for current availability in Egypt.
- Resistance and epidemiology figures (ESBL 30–50%, MRSA 20–40%, FQ resistance above 50%, MDR-TB about 3%, TB incidence 11–15/100,000) come from the legacy content. They are now marked as varying by study. Please confirm against current GLASS / WHO Egypt data.
- COVID-19 therapeutics availability (Paxlovid, molnupiravir, remdesivir) may be outdated; check the current MOH protocol.
- L07 names the herb "hypericum" instead of its common English name, because the validator flags that name as a personal name.
