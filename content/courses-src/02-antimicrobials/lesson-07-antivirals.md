## 1. Influenza antivirals

Neuraminidase inhibitors stop new influenza virus particles from leaving infected cells, so they work best while the virus is still multiplying — early in the illness.

| Drug | Brand | Usual adult dosing | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oseltamivir | Tamiflu | 75mg BD × 5 days | Start within 48h of symptom onset |
| Zanamivir | Relenza (limited) | Inhaled | Within 48h |

**Egypt availability:** Tamiflu is stocked seasonally; a prescription is required. Benefit is greatest when started within 48 hours; doctors may still prescribe it later for high-risk or hospitalised patients. Common side effects are nausea and vomiting — take with food.

**Refer urgently:** breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, persistent high fever, or a high-risk patient (pregnancy, elderly, chronic heart, lung or kidney disease, diabetes, immunosuppression).

## 2. Herpes antivirals

Acyclovir and valacyclovir (a prodrug of acyclovir with better absorption) inhibit viral DNA polymerase.

| Drug | Brand (Egypt) | Indication | Usual adult dosing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acyclovir | Zovirax, Acyclovir generics | HSV, VZV | HSV: 200mg 5 times daily × 5 days; zoster: 800mg 5 times daily × 7 days |
| Valacyclovir | Valtrex, Pharco/Memphis generics | HSV, VZV | Zoster: 1g TDS × 7 days |
| Acyclovir cream | Zovirax cream | Cold sores | Apply 5 times daily at the first tingle |

**Counselling:** start as early as possible — for shingles ideally within 72 hours of the rash appearing. Drink plenty of water. Doses are reduced in kidney disease.

**Refer urgently:** shingles near the eye or on the tip of the nose, rash in an immunocompromised patient, or severe pain with fever.

## 3. COVID-19 therapeutics (Egypt context)

Availability and national protocols for COVID-19 medicines change; follow the current Ministry of Health protocol and the Lotus SOP.

| Agent | Status in Egypt | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Remdesivir | Hospital use | IV — moderate to severe COVID-19 |
| Paxlovid (nirmatrelvir/ritonavir) | Limited / private | Start within 5 days of symptoms; many drug interactions; dose reduced in moderate kidney impairment |
| Molnupiravir | Variable availability | Oral alternative; not used in pregnancy |
| Dexamethasone | Widely available | Only for severe COVID-19 in patients needing oxygen, under medical supervision — not for mild cases or self-medication |

**Paxlovid interactions:** ritonavir strongly inhibits CYP3A4. It is contraindicated with several cardiovascular drugs (for example amiodarone, simvastatin) and interacts with many others. A pharmacist interaction check of every medicine is essential.

## 4. HIV (awareness)

| Class | Examples | Note |
|---|---|---|
| NRTIs | Tenofovir, lamivudine | Fixed combinations are common |
| NNRTIs | Efavirenz | CNS side effects (vivid dreams, dizziness) |
| Integrase inhibitors | Dolutegravir | Preferred regimens; separate from antacids, iron and calcium |

The Egyptian Ministry of Health provides antiretrovirals through designated centres. Community pharmacists need awareness to check interactions (for example rifampicin, antacids, the herbal product hypericum) and to keep strict confidentiality.

**Counselling script (Arabic):**

> "تاميفلو لازم يبدأ في أول 48 ساعة من أعراض الإنفلونزا عشان يكون فعال. زوفيراكس للهربس لازم يتاخد في مواعيده بالظبط. لو عندك كوفيد ودكتور كتب علاج، اسأل الصيدلي عن تعارض الأدوية."

In English: Tamiflu must start within the first 48 hours of flu symptoms to work well. Zovirax for herpes must be taken exactly on time. If you have COVID-19 and a doctor prescribed treatment, ask the pharmacist to check for drug interactions.

## 5. Scenario

> An elderly patient with diabetes, taking atorvastatin 40mg, is prescribed Paxlovid for COVID-19.

**Model response:**

1. Review all medicines for ritonavir interactions, not only the statin
2. Contact the prescriber: atorvastatin is commonly paused during the Paxlovid course and restarted a few days after it ends — agree the plan with the prescriber
3. Check kidney function — Paxlovid dose is reduced in moderate kidney impairment
4. Counsel completion of the 5-day course, with the tablets taken together twice daily
5. Give red flags for urgent care: breathlessness, chest pain, confusion, low oxygen readings

## Key takeaways

- Tamiflu works best when started within 48 hours of influenza symptoms
- Zovirax and Valtrex (acyclovir and valacyclovir) treat HSV and shingles; start early
- Shingles dosing: acyclovir 800mg five times daily or valacyclovir 1g three times daily for 7 days
- Paxlovid has significant drug interactions; pharmacist review is critical
- Remdesivir is a hospital IV treatment for COVID-19 in Egypt
- HIV antiretrovirals are dispensed through Ministry of Health designated centres
- Dexamethasone reduces mortality only in severe COVID-19 needing oxygen
