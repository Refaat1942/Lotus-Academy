## 1. Nuxuriance (45+): deeper lines, day and night

The sheet lists this line for customers aged 45 and above and writes the extra name "Aroma Perfection" in the usage column of the header row. As in Lesson 14.3, copy extra names as written and do not explain them.

| Product | Listed use |
|---|---|
| Day Cream (Normal & Dry Skin) (50 ml) | Cream for deep wrinkles on the face, for daytime |
| Day Cream, Emulsion (Combination Skin) (50 ml) | Cream for deep wrinkles on the face, for daytime |
| Night (All Skin Types) (50 ml) | Cream for deep wrinkles on the face, for night |
| Serum Day/Night (All Skin Types) (30 ml) | Serum for deep wrinkles on the face, night and day, and the eye area |
| Eye And Lips (15 ml) | Serum for deep wrinkles around the eyes and lips |

How to explain this line:

- The line is the **correction** step for customers who already have deeper lines. The language is "deep wrinkles", so remind customers that improvement is gradual and wrinkles are not erased.
- **Day** product depends on skin type: normal and dry skin, or combination skin (emulsion texture).
- **Night** product suits all skin types.
- The **serum** is the flexible product for day and night, face and eye area. The **Eye And Lips** serum targets the delicate area around the eyes and lips.
- General advice: a rich night product does not replace daytime sunscreen. Sunscreen stays the daily first priority.

## 2. Prodigieuse: tired skin, eyes, day and night

The sheet lists Prodigieuse with the words "Brightening Program" in the usage column. The products below are listed with anti-fatigue and skin-tiredness wording. Use the product wording; do not claim more than it says about brightening.

| Product | Listed use |
|---|---|
| Eye Cream (15 ml) | Cream that is anti-fatigue and for puffiness, wrinkles and dark circles |
| Day Cream (40 ml) | Day cream, anti-fatigue and for tired skin |
| Night Cream (40 ml) | Night cream, anti-fatigue and for tired skin, and renewing it |

Talking points:

- Ideal customer: someone whose skin looks tired, for example after long working days, little sleep or a busy household, who wants a day and night care pair and an eye cream.
- The eye cream is listed for puffiness, wrinkles and dark circles. Compare with the ISIS eye product listed for puffiness and dark circles (Lesson 14.1) if the customer asks for options; present both fairly, by listed use and price, with no claim that one is better.
- General advice: dark circles can have several causes (for example heredity, tiredness, allergy). If a customer is worried or the circles appeared suddenly, suggest a medical opinion.

## 3. Huile prodigieuse and the dry oils

In the sheet, "Huile prodigieuse" is a header followed by the items below. The usage column of the header row reads "UV Protector (30) ML" and the next row, "Masque Frais Hydratant (50) ML", has no usage written. The sheet does not make clear how these relate to the line, so keep the wording cautious.

| Product | Listed use |
|---|---|
| UV Protector (30 ml) (written in the usage column of the header row) | The sheet gives the name and size only. Check the leaflet for its use and sun-protection level before advising |
| Masque Frais Hydratant (50 ml) | No usage written in the sheet. The name suggests a fresh hydrating mask; check the leaflet before advising |
| Dry Oil (100 / 50 ml) | Dry oil for face, hair and body, for hydration, nourishment and fighting wrinkles |
| Dry Oil Golden Shimmer (100 / 50 ml) | Bronze-coloured dry oil for face, hair and body, for hydration, nourishment and fighting wrinkles |

Talking points for the dry oils:

- One oil, three uses: face, hair and body. This is the Nuxe product with the widest use, so it suits customers who like a multi-purpose item.
- "Dry oil" is a light-feeling oil that is not meant to feel greasy. Explaining the texture this way is general knowledge; invite the customer to try it on the back of the hand.
- The **Golden Shimmer** version is bronze-coloured. It suits a customer who wants a gentle glow on the skin. Test on a small area first.
- Do not claim that an oil replaces a daily facial sunscreen.
- For oily and acne-prone skin, suggest testing a small area first and ask the customer to stop if the skin reacts (general advice).

## 5. Explaining each Nuxe line by age and need (whole range)

| Line | Age band in sheet | Need | Core message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cleansing steps | All | Daily cleansing | Choose one cleanser by skin type, then toning lotion; exfoliant and mask three times weekly as listed |
| Merveillane | 25-35 | Early expression lines | One cream by skin type; the fluid for combination and oily |
| Aroma Vaillance | 35-45 | Density, targeted lines | Cream emulsion for density; roll-on for forehead, eyes and lips |
| Nuxuriance | 45+ | Deep wrinkles | Day by skin type, night for all, serum and eye and lips |
| Prodigieuse | not stated | Tired skin and eyes | Day, night and eye cream |
| Huile prodigieuse and dry oils | not stated | Hydration and nourishment | Dry oil for face, hair and body; bronze version for glow |
| 3 Roses, Hand & Nail | not stated | Body and hands | Confirm the exact items with the leaflet and the Lotus team |

Remember that the sheet gives age bands only for the three age lines. For the others, ask about the need rather than the age.

### Conversation tips

- Start with the customer's concern, not the product list.
- Offer a maximum of two or three products at a time.
- Present the line that matches the age and need, then the add-on that makes the biggest difference, usually a cleanser and daily sunscreen.
- Check the leaflet in front of the customer for items marked as unclear in this lesson.

## Scenario

A woman in her fifties comes into a Lotus branch carrying a small gift bag for a friend and asking, "What do you have for deep lines?" She has dry skin. The pharmacist asks about her current routine and any sensitivities. The pharmacist presents the Nuxuriance line: the day cream for normal and dry skin, and the night cream, both listed for deep wrinkles, and mentions the Eye And Lips serum listed for deep wrinkles around the eyes and lips. The pharmacist is honest that lines soften gradually, recommends daily sunscreen on top, and explains the three-step plan. The customer asks about the gift. For her friend, a young woman who likes products for the face, hair and body, the pharmacist suggests the dry oil, and offers to check the leaflet for the item the customer saw in the Prodigieuse range. The customer leaves with a clear plan and a gift idea, and does not feel pushed.

## Key takeaways

- Nuxuriance (45+): day cream by skin type, night cream, serum and Eye And Lips for deep wrinkles
- Prodigieuse: anti-fatigue day, night and eye care for tired skin; use the wording of the sheet
- Dry oils are listed for face, hair and body and for hydration, nourishment and fighting wrinkles; the Golden Shimmer version is bronze
- The UV protector, the fresh mask, the body item and the hand item are only partly described in the sheet: check the leaflet and do not guess
- Explain each line by the customer's need first, age band second
- A rich night cream or oil never replaces daily sunscreen
- Offer two or three products at a time and avoid pressure
