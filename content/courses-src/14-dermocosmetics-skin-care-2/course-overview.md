## Course description

This course is for pharmacists and pharmacy staff in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt who already know the basics of dermocosmetic counselling and now want to advise confidently on the more specialist and premium part of the shelf: anti-ageing care, brightening and pigmentation products, and the Nuxe range. It builds on the first dermocosmetics course and focuses on three brands in the Lotus product list: ISIS Pharma, Nuxe and A.C.M. Depiwhite.

Each lesson separates two kinds of content. Product facts come only from the Lotus product sheet and say what each product is for, in the team's own words. General skin-care knowledge, such as why daily sun protection matters during a brightening routine, is added separately and kept conservative. Where the sheet is unclear, the lesson uses cautious wording and tells you to check the product leaflet.

The course moves from concepts to products to selling. Lessons 1 and 2 cover anti-ageing and brightening with the ISIS and A.C.M. products. Lessons 3 and 4 walk through the Nuxe range line by line. Lesson 5 puts everything together into premium morning and evening routines and shows how to handle price objections honestly. Every lesson ends with a 10-question quiz, and the course ends with a 20-question final exam.

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the main causes of fine lines and wrinkles and distinguish prevention from correction in customer counselling
2. Match each ISIS product in the Lotus list to the customer need it is listed for
3. Describe how brightening routines work and why daily sun protection and patience are essential
4. Counsel safely on brightening products, including sensitive-area use, pregnancy and breastfeeding caution, patch testing and dermatologist referral
5. Navigate the Nuxe cleansing, age-targeted, eye and lip, brightening, dry oil and body lines and explain each by age and need
6. Build a simple morning and evening premium routine and explain its expected length and results honestly
7. Handle premium-price objections with honest value explanation, ethical add-ons and clear referral triggers
