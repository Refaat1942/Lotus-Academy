## 1. How the Nuxe section of the sheet is organised

Nuxe is a premium French skin-care brand. In the Lotus sheet the products are laid out in groups:

1. A general cleansing and care group (cleansing water, milk, foam, toning lotion, exfoliant and mask)
2. Age-targeted lines, each with a name and an age band: Merveillane (25-35), Aroma Vaillance (35-45) and Nuxuriance (45+)
3. Prodigieuse (listed with a brightening programme) and Huile prodigieuse (dry oils and related items)
4. A body item, 3 Roses (written as Nuxe Body)

This lesson covers groups 1 and the first two age lines. Lesson 14.4 covers the rest. Everything in the tables below is taken from the sheet. Anything we add about skin care in general is marked as general advice.

> Note on the sheet: in the age-line rows the sheet writes another name in the usage column (for example a second line name). These are copied in the tables exactly as written, and the Lotus team will confirm their meaning. When a customer asks, describe the products by their listed use and avoid explaining the extra names.

## 2. The cleansing and care steps

General principle (general advice, not from the sheet): cleansing is the foundation. Clean skin lets later care steps work comfortably, and cleansing at night removes make-up, sunscreen and the day's dirt.

| Step | Product | Listed use | Who to suggest it to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cleansing Water (100 / 200 ml) | Cleanser, make-up remover, moisturising, for face and eyes | Customers who want a quick, gentle cleanse of face and eyes, including make-up removal |
| 1 | Cleansing Milk (200 ml) | Cleanser, make-up remover, moisturising for the face, dry skin | Dry skin |
| 1 | Cleansing Foam (150 ml) | Cleanser, make-up remover, moisturising through the foam | Customers who like a foaming texture |
| 1 | Cleansing gel (200 ml) | Wash for dry and sensitive skin and make-up remover | Dry or sensitive skin |
| 2 | Toning lotion (200 ml) | Pore-tightening after the cleansing process | After cleansing, as a second step |
| Weekly | Gentle Aromatic Exfoliant (125 ml) | Face scrub, 3 times weekly | Customers who want a smoother surface and accept regular weekly use |
| Weekly | Gentle Purifying Mask (50 ml) | Cleansing mask for the skin, 3 times weekly | Customers who want a purifying treat in their routine |

How to use the table:

- Choose **one** cleanser. Water, milk, foam and gel are alternatives, not a stack. Ask about skin type and texture preference.
- The milk and the gel are the two listed for **dry** skin; the gel also for **sensitive** skin.
- The toning lotion is listed as a pore-tightening step after cleansing.
- The exfoliant and the mask are each listed for **three times weekly**. Say exactly this. For a customer with sensitive skin, suggest starting with fewer uses and increasing gradually, as general advice, and stop if irritation appears.
- Exfoliation always goes with daily sun protection (general advice).

### Sample cleansing routine (general advice built from listed products)

| Time | Steps |
|---|---|
| Morning | Cleanser suited to skin type, toning lotion, then care cream, then sunscreen |
| Evening | Cleanser or make-up remover, toning lotion, then care cream or night product |
| Weekly | Exfoliant and mask as listed, never on irritated skin |

## 3. Merveillane (25-35)

The sheet lists this line for the 25-35 age band. The usage column shows the extra name "Reve De Miel". Treat the extra name as written by the Lotus team and do not explain it.

| Product | Listed use | Skin type |
|---|---|---|
| Cream (All Skin Types) (50 ml) | Cream for expression lines | All skin types |
| Enrichie Cream (Very Dry Skin) (50 ml) | Cream for expression lines | Very dry skin |
| Serum (All Skin Types) (30 ml) | Cream for expression lines; face; eye area; night and day | All skin types |
| Fluid (50 ml) | Cream for expression lines | Combination and oily skin |

Talking points:

- The common thread is **expression lines**, the lines that come from facial movement. These are early, so the message is prevention and gentle correction.
- The only difference between the three creams is skin type, so ask about skin type first: all types, very dry, or combination and oily.
- The serum is listed for face and eye area, day and night. It is a flexible product for customers who want one extra step.
- Be honest: expression lines soften gradually and will not vanish.

## 4. Aroma Vaillance (35-45)

The usage column adds "Hand&Nail (75) ML" on the line header row. The sheet does not make clear whether the hand and nail item belongs to this line, so it is covered in Lesson 14.4 as a listed item with cautious wording.

| Product | Listed use |
|---|---|
| Cleansing gel (200 ml) | Wash for dry and sensitive skin and make-up remover |
| Cream (Emulsion) (50 ml) | Cream to restore density for normal and combination skin |
| Roll On (15 ml) | A pen for the forehead, the eye area and the area around the lips |

Talking points:

- "Restore density" is the sheet's phrase. In conversation say "listed to help restore the density of normal and combination skin" and avoid describing how it works.
- The roll-on is a targeted pen for three areas: the forehead, around the eyes and around the lips. It suits a customer who wants to treat only those areas.
- The cleansing gel sits in the 35-45 group in the sheet and is also in the general cleansing table above. It is one product; count it once when you build a routine.

## 5. Explaining Nuxe by age and need (part 1)

| Customer | Suggested conversation |
|---|---|
| Late twenties, wants early lines care, normal skin | Merveillane cream (all skin types) listed for expression lines, plus cleanser and daily sunscreen |
| Early thirties, very dry skin | Merveillane Enrichie cream listed for very dry skin |
| Combination or oily skin, early lines | Merveillane fluid |
| Late thirties, normal or combination skin, wants density | Aroma Vaillance cream emulsion |
| Wants a precise product for forehead, eyes and lips | Aroma Vaillance roll-on |

Age bands are a guide. Ask about needs, not only age.

## Scenario

A customer in her early thirties with combination skin tells the pharmacist she wants "something French and good" because she has started to see lines on her forehead when she frowns. She currently uses face wipes. The pharmacist asks about her routine and sensitivity, explains that wipes are not a full cleansing step, and offers a cleansing water or foam, then the toning lotion, and a day product from the Merveillane line listed for combination and oily skin (the fluid). The pharmacist also mentions the exfoliant three times weekly as listed, with a gentle start, and daily sunscreen. The customer asks about the price; the pharmacist explains each step simply and offers to start with the cleanser and the fluid only. She leaves with a clear two-step plan and no pressure to buy the full range.

## Key takeaways

- Choose one cleanser: water, milk, foam or gel, according to skin type and preference
- Milk and gel are listed for dry skin; gel also for sensitive skin
- Toning lotion follows cleansing; exfoliant and mask are listed for three times weekly
- Merveillane (25-35): four products for expression lines, differing by skin type
- Aroma Vaillance (35-45): cleansing gel, cream emulsion for density and a roll-on pen for forehead, eyes and lips
- Ages are a guide; ask about needs and sensitivity
- Where the sheet is unclear, use cautious wording and confirm with the Lotus team
