## Learning objectives

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

1. List every Foltène item in the Lotus product notes and state what each one is for
2. Separate the hair-loss items from the anti-dandruff items and the care items
3. Explain the difference between a shampoo and a treatment in the range, using only the Lotus product notes
4. Counsel a customer on the lashes and nails items without overpromising
5. Combine the Foltène items into a simple, honest routine for a customer
6. Recognise the limits of the product notes and refer when a problem is outside them

---

## 1. How to use the product facts in this lesson

All product information in this lesson comes from the **Lotus product notes** (the usage sheet prepared by the Lotus team for the Foltène range). The notes give a short usage line for each item. They do not list ingredients, percentages, ages or detailed directions, so this lesson does not add any. For directions, frequency and suitability, always read the label and the leaflet in the box, and follow the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority / Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

General hair and scalp knowledge (the hair cycle, causes of shedding, red flags) is from Lesson 15.1 and is kept separate from product facts.

## 3. Hair loss items

### Shampoo Men and Shampoo Women (200 ml)

The notes describe both as nourishing the hair and preventing hair loss. A shampoo is the daily or regular wash step, so it is the easiest way to start a routine. The notes do not describe any difference between the men's and women's versions beyond the name, so choose according to who will use it, and read the label.

### Treatment Men and Treatment Women (100 ml)

The notes describe these as ampoules containing a substance that prevents hair loss. They are the "intensive" step, used in addition to the shampoo when the customer has a real shedding concern. Do not state the name of the substance, the concentration or the number of weeks: the notes do not give them. Read the leaflet and give the customer the directions printed there.

**How to present the pair:**

> Pharmacist: "The shampoo is for the regular wash and the treatment is the extra step. Together they give your scalp a consistent routine. Hair needs months of regular use before we judge, so let's plan to review in a few months."

### Counselling points for the hair loss items

- Ask first: shedding, thinning or breakage? (Lesson 15.1)
- Ask who it is for, and choose the men's or women's version accordingly.
- Explain that these are products to support hair and scalp; they do not replace a doctor's check for sudden, patchy or persistent loss.
- Avoid words such as "cures", "stops completely" or "guaranteed". Say "the product notes describe it as preventing hair loss".

## 4. Anti-dandruff items

| Item | Use | When to suggest |
|---|---|---|
| Shampoo Anti Dandruff | Shampoo against dandruff | Customer with flakes and itching, first step |
| Treatment Anti Dandruff | Ampoules against dandruff | Customer who wants an additional step or whose dandruff returns |

Check first that it is really dandruff (Lesson 15.1). Larger greasy flakes with itching point to dandruff. A very red, thick, silvery, painful or oozing scalp should be seen by a dermatologist before any shampoo is chosen.

Explain to the customer that dandruff tends to return when treatment stops completely, so ask them to follow the label and keep the routine long enough, rather than stopping at the first improvement.

## 5. Care, lashes and nails

### Conditioner

The notes list it as a conditioner. Its role is the last step after shampooing, to make hair easier to comb and softer. It is a care item, not a hair-loss treatment, so do not sell it as one. It is a natural and honest add-on for a customer buying the shampoo.

### Eyelash Treatment

The notes describe a mascara to strengthen and treat the eyelashes. Counselling points:

- Do not promise longer or thicker lashes. Say what the notes say: it is described as a lash-strengthening mascara.
- Remind the customer to follow the label for use and removal.
- If the customer has eye irritation, redness or swelling, she should stop using it and see a doctor or pharmacist for advice. Be extra careful with anyone who wears contact lenses or has known eye allergy.

### Nail treatment

The notes describe a nail strengthener. Ask about the cause: brittle nails from frequent water contact or cosmetics are different from nails with pitting, thickness or colour changes, which need a doctor.

## 6. Building a simple Foltène routine

| Customer need | Suggested items from the range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shedding concern (man) | Shampoo Men, plus Treatment Men if more support is wanted | Review in a few months; doctor check if persistent |
| Shedding concern (woman) | Shampoo Women, plus Treatment Women | Same as above |
| Dandruff | Shampoo Anti Dandruff, plus Treatment Anti Dandruff if needed | Do not stop at first improvement |
| Softer, easier hair | Conditioner | Care only |
| Weak lashes | Eyelash Treatment | Follow label, stop if irritation |
| Weak nails | Nail treatment | Refer if nails look abnormal |

## 7. Common mistakes

- Using the men's or women's wording to make claims that are not in the notes
- Selling the anti-dandruff shampoo to a customer with a dry, tight scalp and no flakes
- Promising lash growth
- Forgetting that the conditioner is a care product, not a treatment
- Not explaining that ampoules are an extra step and that months of regular use are needed

## Scenario

A man in his forties comes in on his way home from work. He is worried about shedding and also has some flakes on his collar. He wants "one thing" for both and has little time.

**Model approach:**

1. Pharmacist asks about duration, pattern (all over or patches) and scalp symptoms. The shedding is gradual, all over, and the scalp is not painful or very red.
2. The pharmacist explains that there are two separate concerns: shedding and flakes.
3. The pharmacist suggests starting with one product for the main concern, for example the Men shampoo from the range (described as nourishing and preventing hair loss), and discusses whether to add the anti-dandruff shampoo for the flakes, to be used as directed on the labels.
4. The pharmacist explains that hair needs months of regular use, and that if shedding increases or becomes patchy, or the scalp becomes red or painful, he should see a dermatologist.
5. The pharmacist writes down the plan and offers a follow-up visit.

## Key takeaways

- The Foltène notes list nine items: shampoos, ampoule treatments, a conditioner, an eyelash mascara and a nail strengthener
- Hair loss: Shampoo Men, Shampoo Women, Treatment Men, Treatment Women
- Dandruff: Shampoo Anti Dandruff and Treatment Anti Dandruff (ampoules)
- Conditioner is a care step, the eyelash item is a mascara to strengthen lashes, and the nail item is a nail strengthener
- Use only what the Lotus product notes say, and read the label for directions
- Never promise guaranteed results; set a months-long expectation for hair loss
- Refer when loss is sudden or patchy, or when the scalp is painful, very red or scaly
