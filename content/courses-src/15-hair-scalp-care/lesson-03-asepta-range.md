## Learning objectives

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

1. List every Asepta item in the Lotus product notes and state what each one is for
2. Distinguish the hair-loss items (ampoules, lotion, cream) from the hair-care items (shampoo, mask, conditioner)
3. Explain the treatment duration stated in the notes and why the customer should not stop early
4. Present the statements about the ANP ingredient and safety accurately, attributing them to the Lotus product notes
5. Counsel on the eyelash and nail items without overpromising
6. Decide when a customer with patchy hair loss needs a doctor before or alongside any product

## 1. Sources and how to speak about this range

The facts in this lesson come from the **Lotus product notes** for Asepta. The Lotus sheet lists the hair-loss line under the name **Ecrinal**. It also contains three notes at the end of the sheet: one about treatment duration, and two about the ANP ingredient and safety.

When you pass these facts to a customer, attribute them: "According to the Lotus product notes ..." This keeps you accurate and stops you from adding promises of your own. Ingredients, percentages, ages and directions that are not in the notes are not given in this lesson. For directions and suitability, read the label and leaflet, and follow the rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority / Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

## 3. Hair-loss items

### Ecrinal Ampouls and Ecrinal Lotion

Both are described as "for all cases of hair loss". That is a broad statement. In practice, you still ask the questions from Lesson 15.1 and refer when there are red flags, because loss has many different causes and a product does not replace finding the cause when the loss is sudden, rapid or persistent. A reasonable way to say it:

> Pharmacist: "The Lotus product notes list these ampoules and the lotion for hair loss. They are used regularly over months, and if the loss is sudden or keeps going, a doctor should check the cause."

The notes do not say how to use the ampoules or the lotion, how many times a week, or the volume. Follow the leaflet.

### Ecrinal Cream: patches

The notes describe the cream for cases of hair loss in areas or patches on the head. This is where you need the most care. **Sudden round or smooth patches, or patches in a child, should be seen by a doctor or dermatologist.** The cream may be offered as supportive care after the customer understands this and, if needed, after the doctor has seen the problem. Do not diagnose the cause of the patches.

### The note on treatment duration

At the end of the sheet the Lotus notes state: hair-loss cases need **3 months of treatment**, and the patient must not stop before this period.

How to use this honestly:

- Say it clearly before the sale: "The Lotus notes say the treatment period for hair loss is 3 months and you should not stop before it."
- Plan the purchase around it. A customer who knows it is a 3-month plan can decide on the number of boxes and a reminder date, instead of stopping after the first box.
- Do not translate it into a promise of results at 3 months. The period is how long to continue, not a guarantee.

### The ANP statements

The final notes in the sheet describe the **ANP ingredient** as follows (restated from the notes):

- It is a natural substance extracted from horse neck hair.
- It helps increase keratin, which the notes say represents 90% of the protein inside the hair.
- The notes state that this prevents the hair from falling out completely, and restores its growth within only two months.
- The notes state that it is safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding and for children.

These are strong statements. Use them carefully:

- Always begin with "The Lotus product notes state ..." and do not repeat the strongest wording as your own guarantee.
- The same sheet says that treatment needs 3 months. Explain to the customer that the 3-month period is how long to continue, and do not promise a result at two months.
- On pregnancy, breastfeeding and children, you may say what the notes state, and also suggest that the customer reads the label and checks with her doctor, especially with a pregnant or breastfeeding mother or a child. Hair loss after childbirth is very common and these customers will ask.
- Do not enlarge them. Ingredients in the other items and the exact form of ANP in each product are not given in the notes.

## 4. Hair-care items

### Ecrinal Shampoo

The notes describe it as free from chemical substances. Treat this wording with caution. Say "the Lotus notes describe it as free from chemical substances" rather than "completely harmless" or "natural". Any new product can still cause sensitivity in some people, so advise the customer to stop and ask for advice if burning, redness or rash occurs.

### Ecrinal Mask

A "cream bath" for damaged and split hair. This fits breakage and split ends (Lesson 15.1), not shedding. Suggest it after shampooing, as directed on the label.

### Ecrinal Conditioner

Used after washing with shampoo, to give softness and shine to the hair. A care step, like the Mask, and a natural add-on for a customer buying the shampoo.

## 5. Eyelash and nail items

| Item | Notes say | Counselling |
|---|---|---|
| Eyelash | Mascara | Follow the label. Stop and seek advice if the eye becomes red, irritated or swollen. No promises of longer lashes. |
| Nail strengthener | Strengthens the nails | Ask the cause of weak nails. Abnormal colour, thickness or pitting needs a doctor. |
| Nail Fortifying cream | Cream for nail cracks and the skin growths around the nails | The wording about "skin growths" is unclear in the sheet (it may refer to hangnails or skin around the nail). Describe it as the sheet does and read the label. If the skin around the nail is red, painful or has pus, the customer needs a doctor. |

## 6. Comparison: which Asepta item for which customer

| Customer says | Suggested Asepta item | Remember |
|---|---|---|
| "My hair is falling all over" | Ecrinal Ampouls and/or Ecrinal Lotion | Explain the 3-month period; doctor if sudden or persistent |
| "I have a bald spot" | Doctor or dermatologist first. Ecrinal Cream as supportive care if appropriate | Never diagnose; child with patches goes to a doctor |
| "My hair is dry and splitting" | Ecrinal Mask, Ecrinal Conditioner | Care, not hair-loss |
| "I want a gentle shampoo" | Ecrinal Shampoo | Do not claim harmless; sensitivity caution |
| "My nails crack" | Nail strengthener, Nail Fortifying cream | Refer if skin or nail looks infected or abnormal |
| "Weak lashes" | Eyelash mascara | No promises |

## Scenario

A mother, three months after childbirth and breastfeeding, tells the pharmacist that she is losing a lot of hair when she brushes. She asks whether any product is safe for her.

**Model approach:**

1. Pharmacist reassures that shedding after childbirth is common, and asks about duration, patches, general health and tiredness.
2. She has diffuse shedding, no patches. The pharmacist offers the Ecrinal Ampouls and/or Lotion from the hair-loss line.
3. The pharmacist says: "According to the Lotus product notes, the ingredient ANP is stated to be safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Please also read the leaflet, and if you wish, mention it to your doctor."
4. The pharmacist explains the 3-month period stated in the notes and agrees a plan and a review date.
5. The pharmacist adds that she should see a doctor if the shedding is sudden, in patches, or comes with tiredness or other symptoms, and offers the Ecrinal Conditioner as a gentle care add-on.

## Key takeaways

- The Asepta list has nine items: Ecrinal Ampouls, Lotion, Cream, Shampoo, Mask, Conditioner, Eyelash mascara, Nail strengthener and Nail Fortifying cream
- Ampouls and Lotion are listed for all cases of hair loss; the Cream is for loss in areas or patches
- The notes state a hair-loss treatment period of 3 months, and that the patient should not stop before it
- The ANP statements come from the Lotus notes; attribute them and do not turn them into guarantees
- The Mask, Conditioner and Shampoo are care items, not hair-loss treatments
- Patchy or sudden loss needs a doctor or dermatologist, with product as support at most
- Always add the sensitivity caution and follow the label
