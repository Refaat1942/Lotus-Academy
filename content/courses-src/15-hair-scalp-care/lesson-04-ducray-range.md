## Learning objectives

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

1. List every Ducray item in the Lotus product notes and state what each one is for
2. Group the items by need: hair loss, dandruff, psoriasis-type scalp, oily scalp and damaged ends
3. Restate the Neoptide dosing line exactly as the Lotus notes give it, and explain the arithmetic of the box
4. Explain the seasonal use of the Elution shampoo as stated in the notes, without adding details
5. Choose between the three Nutriceret items for damaged and split ends
6. Recognise the limits of the notes and refer when a scalp problem may be more than dandruff

---

## 1. Sources and approach

All product facts in this lesson come from the **Lotus product notes** for Ducray. The notes give a one-line use for each item. They do not give ingredients, concentrations, ages or frequency for most items, and this lesson does not add any. The only item with a dosing line is Neoptide, which is restated as the notes give it. For all other directions read the label and leaflet, and follow the rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority / Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

Some product forms are not stated in the notes (for example whether a particular item is a shampoo, a lotion or a cream). Where the form is not stated, this lesson says so and does not guess.

## 3. Hair loss

### Anastim

Described as ampoules for treating hair loss that results from weakness of the hair follicle. Notice that the notes link it to a cause (weak follicle). You may not say what the cause is in a particular customer; that is for a doctor if you suspect it is something else. Ask the Lesson 15.1 questions first.

### Anaphase

The notes say only "hair loss". The form (shampoo, ampoule, capsule or other) is not stated, so check the box in front of you and read the label. Do not describe how to use it from memory.

### Neoptide

Described as being for treating chronic hair loss in women. The Lotus notes include a dosing line, restated here as the notes give it:

- A box contains **3 sprays, each 30 ml, for 3 months**.
- Used **once daily**.
- **1 ml = 12 sprays**, for one month.
- Sprayed **in the evening on dry hair**, and the hair is **washed two hours later**.

The arithmetic fits the notes: at 1 ml per day, one 30 ml spray bottle lasts about a month, so three bottles cover the three months. This also matches the 3-month treatment period that the Lotus notes mention for Asepta hair-loss cases (Lesson 15.3). Neoptide's line, however, appears under Ducray, so describe it as "the Lotus notes for Ducray".

Part of the dosing line is hard to read in the original sheet (the wording around "for a month" is unclear), so for exact directions always follow the leaflet in the box. This is listed for the Lotus team in the author notes.

Counselling points:

- It is described for women with chronic loss. For a man or a child, do not extend the notes on your own: offer another listed hair-loss item or refer.
- A customer who sprays at night and washes two hours later should plan the routine, for example a regular evening time that leaves two hours before washing.
- Chronic loss that has not been checked by a doctor is a reason to suggest a medical check, since there may be an underlying cause.

## 4. Dandruff, psoriasis-type scalp and the seasonal item

### Kelual

For dandruff. The form is not stated in the notes. Use the dandruff questions from Lesson 15.1 and read the label for directions.

### Kertoyl

A shampoo for psoriasis, per the notes. Be careful with wording. You are not diagnosing psoriasis. A customer may say "my doctor told me I have scalp psoriasis" and ask for the right shampoo, and this is the item the notes list. If a customer has thick, silvery plaques and has never been examined, send them to a dermatologist first, and offer to help afterwards.

### Elution shampoo and seasonal use

The notes say it is used by people who suffer from heavy dandruff throughout the winter, so it is preferable to use it during the **3 months of the summer period** to protect against winter dandruff, and it is described as protecting from dandruff and psoriasis.

How to use it honestly:

- Say what the notes say. They suggest use in the summer period, for 3 months, as a preventive step for people who get heavy dandruff in winter.
- The sheet does not say how often to use it within those months, and the exact meaning of "the 3 months of the summer period" is slightly ambiguous. Tell the customer to follow the label, and do not invent a schedule.
- Remind the customer that if the scalp is very red, painful or thick, it needs a dermatologist, not only a shampoo.

## 5. Oily scalp

### Argeal

For oily hair. According to the notes it regulates oil secretion, and therefore prevents dandruff from appearing. The point for counselling is that some flakes are linked to greasy roots; for a customer whose roots are oily within a day or two and who has flakes, this fits. For a dry, tight scalp it does not.

## 6. Damaged and split ends: Nutriceret

Three items in the same family. All three are about broken hair ends:

| Item | Role | Suggest when |
|---|---|---|
| Nutriceret shampoo | Wash step, for broken hair ends | Starting a routine for damaged ends |
| Nutriceret Mask | Treats breakage and dryness, nourishes the hair | The hair is dry and brittle and the customer wants an intensive step |
| Nutriceret emulsion | Leave-in conditioner that treats broken ends | Customer wants a light step that stays in the hair between washes |

Layering is simple: shampoo to wash, the mask as the intensive step, the leave-in emulsion as the finishing care. Explain that split ends cannot grow back whole, so regular trimming also helps. These items are for damaged hair, not for shedding (Lesson 15.1).

## 7. Choosing quickly

| Customer says | First Ducray item to consider | Check |
|---|---|---|
| "Hair falling, follicles weak" | Anastim | Questions of Lesson 15.1; doctor if sudden or patchy |
| "A woman with long-standing hair loss" | Neoptide | Follow the leaflet; consider a doctor check |
| "Flakes and itching" | Kelual | Is it really dandruff? |
| "Heavy dandruff every winter" | Elution shampoo (seasonal use) | Follow the label; summer use per the notes |
| "My doctor said psoriasis of the scalp" | Kertoyl | Do not diagnose; refer if never examined |
| "Greasy roots and flakes" | Argeal | Not for a dry, tight scalp |
| "Broken, dry ends" | Nutriceret shampoo, Mask, emulsion | Care, not a hair-loss item |

## Scenario

A woman in her forties comes to the pharmacy. She has had thinning for years, and a friend recommended a spray. She asks what Ducray has. She is not pregnant. She says her doctor told her years ago it was hormonal and she never followed up.

**Model approach:**

1. Pharmacist asks about duration, pattern and recent changes. The thinning is slow, all over, with no pain or patches.
2. The pharmacist explains that the Lotus notes list Neoptide for chronic hair loss in women. A box has 3 sprays of 30 ml covering 3 months. Directions: once daily in the evening on dry hair and wash two hours later, with the leaflet as the final reference.
3. The pharmacist explains that results need months of regular use and that the customer should plan the evenings, for example setting a daily reminder.
4. The pharmacist suggests a repeat check with her doctor, because chronic loss can have underlying causes and the earlier advice is years old.
5. The pharmacist offers a follow-up date and a gentle care item if needed, without pushing extras.

## Key takeaways

- The Ducray notes list ten items: Anastim, Kertoyl, Kelual, Argeal, Anaphase, Nutriceret emulsion, Nutriceret shampoo, Nutriceret Mask, Elution shampoo and Neoptide
- Hair loss: Anastim (weak follicle), Anaphase (hair loss) and Neoptide (chronic loss in women)
- Dandruff: Kelual and Elution shampoo; Kertoyl is the shampoo listed for psoriasis
- Argeal regulates oil secretion and is for oily hair, so it prevents dandruff from appearing
- Nutriceret emulsion, shampoo and Mask are for broken ends and dry hair, not for shedding
- Neoptide: 3 sprays of 30 ml for 3 months, once daily, 1 ml = 12 sprays, evening on dry hair, wash after two hours (as in the Lotus notes)
- Elution shampoo: seasonal, preferably used in the 3 summer months to protect from winter dandruff (as in the Lotus notes)
