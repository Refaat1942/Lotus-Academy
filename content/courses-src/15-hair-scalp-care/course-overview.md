## Course description

This course is for pharmacists, pharmacy technicians and sales staff in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt who advise customers on hair and scalp care. Customers ask every day about shedding, thinning, dandruff, oily or itchy scalps, dry and broken ends, and sometimes about lashes and nails. The course gives you a simple, safe way to understand the problem, ask the right questions, recognise when a doctor is needed, and match the customer to the right product.

Product knowledge comes from the Lotus product notes for three ranges: Foltène, Asepta (Ecrinal) and Ducray. Every item in those notes is covered with what the notes say it is for. The course does not add claims, ingredients, percentages or ages that the notes do not contain, and it keeps general hair-and-scalp knowledge clearly separate from product facts.

The course has five lessons. Lesson 1 covers hair and scalp basics and the consultation. Lessons 2, 3 and 4 cover the Foltène, Asepta and Ducray ranges. Lesson 5 brings everything together with comparison tables across brands, model consultation dialogues, honest explanation of treatment duration, ethical add-ons, objections and referral. Each lesson ends with a ten-question quiz, and the course ends with a twenty-question final exam. Each lesson also has a video script for the Lotus team to record.

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the hair growth cycle in simple words and set honest expectations about timelines
2. Distinguish shedding, thinning and breakage, and dandruff, dry scalp and oily scalp, through a short consultation
3. Recognise red flags that require referral to a doctor or dermatologist
4. Describe every product in the Foltène, Asepta and Ducray notes and what the Lotus notes say it is for
5. Match a customer's need to the right product across the three ranges using comparison tables
6. Explain treatment duration and seasonal use accurately, attributing them to the Lotus product notes
7. Offer ethical add-ons, handle objections calmly, and close each consultation with a plan and follow-up
