# Author notes — Course 7 (EG-MED-07) upgrade

Not imported. Corrections made to the original content and points the Lotus team should verify.

## Identity kept
- Course ID EG-MED-07, lesson IDs EG-MED-07-L01 to L06, file names, H1 titles, duration (10 hours / 90 min per lesson) and pass mark (70%) unchanged. Added `Language: English and Arabic`.
- Removed the References lists and "Next lesson" links (the format does not allow links; references were generic).
- Overview: outcomes expanded from 5 to 6 (vitamins/iron outcome added so every lesson is covered). The original outcome mentioned "Velosef syrup", but Velosef is cefradine, while the lessons use Ceporex (cefalexin). The wording now says "cephalosporin syrups" — please confirm which brand you want named.

## Corrections
1. **Lesson 7.1 formulation table:** "Syrup 250 mg/5 mL — paracetamol, antibiotics" implied one fixed strength. Changed to "strength varies, e.g. 120 mg/5 mL or 250 mg/5 mL" and added a worked example showing the risk of mixing strengths.
2. **Lesson 7.1 prerequisites:** "See course overview" (no prerequisites are defined there) changed to "None".
3. **Lesson 7.2 Augmentin ES:** "45 mg/kg amox component" was ambiguous. Corrected to 90 mg/kg/day of the amoxicillin component in 2 divided doses (45 mg/kg per dose). Please verify against the current Egyptian leaflet.
4. **Lesson 7.2 ibuprofen:** kept "typically from 6 months" (conservative); some products are licensed from 3 months above a minimum weight. Staff are told to follow the leaflet.
5. **Lesson 7.3 codeine:** "banned pediatrics" changed to: contraindicated for cough under 12 and not recommended up to 18 by international regulators; follow current EDA rules. Please confirm the current EDA status in Egypt.
6. **Lesson 7.3 desloratadine:** "> 6 months" changed to "from the minimum age stated on the product leaflet" (EU-licensed syrup is from 1 year; some markets allow 6 months).
7. **Lesson 7.3 montelukast:** clarified it is a preventer, not a reliever, and added the advice to report sleep, mood or behaviour changes.
8. **Lesson 7.4 ORS:** the Arabic counselling line "dissolve in 1 litre of clean water" was unsafe as a general rule because sachet volumes differ (e.g. 200 mL vs 1 L). Changed to "dissolve in exactly the volume written on the sachet" in both the English and Arabic text.
9. **Lesson 7.4 prerequisites:** "Lessons 7.1–7.4" (lesson listed as its own prerequisite) changed to 7.1–7.3. **Lesson 7.5** changed from 7.1–7.5 to 7.1–7.4.
10. **Lesson 7.5 iron overdose scenario:** "vomiting" was ambiguous and could be read as "induce vomiting". Now says: emergency / poison centre immediately, do NOT induce vomiting, bring the pack; hospitals may give deferoxamine.
11. **Lesson 7.6 MMR timing:** the original said measles/MMR at 9 months and MMR at "9 and 18 months" (quiz Q3 and key takeaways). Current MOH schedules give MMR at 12 and 18 months; the lesson and Q3 now say 12 and 18 months, mention that older schedules used measles at 9 months, and tell staff to check the child's card. **Please verify against the current MOH schedule.**
12. **Lesson 7.6 rotavirus and PCV:** the original listed both as part of the 2/4/6-month MOH schedule (quiz Q7 "Rotavirus: in schedule"). I am not certain both are in the national programme in every governorate now, so the text says "where included in the current MOH programme or offered privately", and Q7 now tests what the vaccine is (oral, against rotavirus diarrhoea) and the need to confirm availability. Added the oral polio zero dose at birth. **Please verify.**
13. **Fever counselling (7.2):** the original Arabic line "paracetamol if distressing or above 38.5" is kept, with the added emphasis that the child's comfort matters more than the number. Fever red flags were added, including any fever under 3 months.
