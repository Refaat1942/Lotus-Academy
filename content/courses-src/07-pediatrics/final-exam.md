# Final Exam — Pediatric Medications in Egypt

## Final Exam (20 questions)

**Q1.** A 12 kg child needs paracetamol at 10 mg/kg per dose. The syrup is 120 mg/5 mL (24 mg/mL). What volume is correct?
- a) 2.5 mL
- b) 10 mL
- c) 5 mL ✓
- d) 12 mL

**Q2.** A mother has two paracetamol products at home, a 120 mg/5 mL syrup and a 250 mg/5 mL syrup, and gives "5 mL" from whichever bottle she finds. What is the key counselling point?
- a) The strengths differ, so the same volume gives a different dose; use one product and calculate its volume by weight ✓
- b) Both syrups are identical, so either is fine
- c) Alternate the two bottles to balance the dose
- d) Always use the stronger bottle for faster relief

**Q3.** A father phones the branch at night asking how much antibiotic suspension to give, but he cannot tell the child's weight. What is the safest action?
- a) Give the adult dose divided by two
- b) Guess the weight from the child's age
- c) Tell him to use a kitchen spoon twice a day
- d) Ask him to check the weight (or weigh the child) and confirm the dose with the prescription and leaflet before giving it ✓

**Q4.** A 9-month-old is vomiting every oral dose of paracetamol and is uncomfortable with fever. Which option is most appropriate?

- b) A paracetamol suppository dosed by weight, with advice to see a doctor if vomiting continues or red flags appear ✓
- c) Crush an adult tablet into water
- d) Give double the oral dose next time

**Q5.** A prescription for Augmentin ES is brought for a 15 kg child with otitis media, but only a standard-strength Augmentin suspension is in stock. What should the pharmacist do?

- b) Do not substitute without recalculating on the amoxicillin component and checking with the prescriber ✓
- c) Dispense cefalexin instead without telling anyone
- d) Double the volume of the standard strength on your own decision

**Q6.** A mother asks whether to give her 6-year-old's leftover aspirin for chickenpox fever. What is the correct advice?
- a) Aspirin is fine for chickenpox in children
- b) Give half an aspirin with milk

- d) Never give aspirin to children under 16 because of Reye syndrome; paracetamol by weight is suitable if uncomfortable ✓

**Q7.** A 2-month-old baby has a temperature of 38.3°C. The mother asks for fever drops. What is the right response?

**Q8.** A mother asks for a cough and cold combination syrup containing an antihistamine and decongestant for her 4-year-old with a cold. What is best?

- b) Sell it with a codeine syrup for night-time

**Q9.** A child prescribed Ventolin inhaler keeps "spraying into the air" and does not improve. What counselling is most useful?

- c) Double the number of puffs without a spacer

**Q10.** A father brings his 18-month-old with cough, fast breathing and the skin between the ribs pulling in. He asks for a syrup. What do you do?
- a) Sell a sedating antihistamine to help sleep
- b) Recommend saline drops and review next week
- c) Sell honey and lemon
- d) Explain that the child needs urgent medical assessment because of breathing difficulty ✓

**Q11.** A mother wants to prepare ORS from a sachet labelled "dissolve in 200 mL of water" using a 1-litre bottle "to make it last longer". What is the correct advice?
