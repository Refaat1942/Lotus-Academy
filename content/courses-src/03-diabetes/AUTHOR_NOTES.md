## Clinical corrections and clarifications
- **Hypoglycaemia (Lesson 3.3 scenario and rule of 15):** the original said "give 15 g glucose immediately" to a confused patient. Changed to: give oral glucose only if the patient is conscious and can swallow safely. Otherwise put nothing in the mouth, give glucagon if available, and call emergency services. Added that hypoglycaemia caused by a sulfonylurea can come back, so the patient should not go home alone.
- **"3 glucose tabs" = 15 g:** changed to "glucose tablets totalling 15 g (check the label; often 3–4)", because tablet strength varies.
- **Sulfonylurea table:** the column called "Duration" actually listed doses. It is now called "Usual dose".
- **Insulin storage (Lesson 3.5 scenario):** the original said "not freezer door", which was confusing. Changed to: store in the main compartment, away from the freezer and the back cooling plate; the door shelf is acceptable.
- **Insulin table:** added a note that Tresiba lasts longer than 24 hours. The in-use period is now "28–42 days depending on the product leaflet".
- **Metformin renal table:** added "initiation generally not recommended" for eGFR 30–45. Added vitamin B12 counselling, sick-day holding and contrast restart after about 48 hours once kidney function is confirmed.
- **SGLT2i:** added sick-day and pre-surgery pause and ketoacidosis with near-normal glucose. **GLP-1 RA:** added the MTC/MEN2 contraindication and "prescription only, not for cosmetic weight loss".
- **HbA1c frequency (Lesson 3.6):** kept "every 3 months" and added "or as the doctor advises (often every 6 months once stable at target)".
- **Foot care:** removed the phrase "Egypt barefoot culture" (it could be read as a stereotype). Replaced it with practical advice to avoid walking barefoot at home, at the mosque and on hot surfaces.
- **Lesson 3.1 Arabic script:** "واحد من كل 7" was changed to "تقريبًا واحد من كل 6 أو 7" to match 15.6%. Added that the physician sets the individual target.
- **LDL target:** kept <70 mg/dL for high risk and added that the physician may set a lower target in established ASCVD.

## Doubts for the Lotus team to verify
1. **Prevalence 15.6% "IDF Diabetes Atlas 2021":** I believe the IDF 2021 edition reported a higher figure for Egypt (about 20%), and 15.6% may come from an earlier edition. The year has been removed and the figure kept as an "IDF estimate". Please confirm against the current IDF Atlas.
2. **Metformin manufacturers:** please verify Cidophage = EIPICO (I believe Cidophage may be made by CID), Formit = Pharco and Gliformin = Memphis. The table is unchanged, but no quiz question depends on the manufacturer.
3. **All EGP prices** (metformin, SGLT2i, GLP-1, insulin, strips "1–3 EGP each", Libre sensors) are from the legacy course and are probably out of date, especially strips. They are labelled "approximate; check current price". Please update or remove them.
4. **Availability:** please confirm the current Egyptian availability of Minidiab (glipizide), Apidra, Levemir (discontinued in some markets), Basaglar, FreeStyle Libre and local insulin biosimilars.
5. **Legacy reference error (removed):** "Zinman B EMPEROR-Reduced NEJM 2020" was wrong. EMPEROR-Reduced was led by Packer (NEJM 2020); Zinman led EMPA-REG OUTCOME (NEJM 2015). The trial name is still mentioned in the lesson, but the lesson has no references.
6. **Pharmacy glucose screening thresholds:** the lessons refer to "the Lotus SOP" and do not state numeric cut-offs. Please make sure such an SOP exists.
