## Course description

This course is for pharmacists and pharmacy staff in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt who dispense diabetes medicines and counsel people living with diabetes every day. Diabetes is one of the most common chronic diseases seen at the Egyptian pharmacy counter: International Diabetes Federation (IDF) estimates have placed adult prevalence in Egypt at about 15.6%, and many people are still undiagnosed. The course gives you practical, conservative pharmacotherapy knowledge for the Egyptian market so that every refill becomes a chance to improve safety and adherence.

The course follows the usual treatment journey of type 2 diabetes. It starts with epidemiology and treatment targets, then covers metformin, sulfonylureas and hypoglycaemia, the newer classes (DPP-4 inhibitors, SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP-1 receptor agonists), insulin types, brands and storage, and finally glucose meters, monitoring, foot care and integrated counselling. Each lesson includes tables of commonly available brands, Arabic counselling phrases, red flags for referral and a realistic pharmacy scenario.

Each lesson takes about 90 minutes and ends with a 10-question quiz, and a 20-question final exam covers the whole course. Pharmacists counsel, check and refer; they do not diagnose or change a prescribed regimen. Prices in this course are approximate and change often: always check the current price. Follow the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority and the Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

## Learning outcomes

1. State **Egyptian diabetes targets** (HbA1c <7% for most adults) and explain why targets are individualised
2. Master **metformin** brands, formulations and renal dose adjustment
3. Manage **hypoglycaemia** from sulfonylureas (Diamicron, Glibenclamide) using the rule of 15 and clear referral triggers
4. Counsel on **SGLT2i/GLP-1** — Forxiga, Jardiance, Ozempic — and on DPP-4 inhibitors
5. Identify **insulin brands** — Lantus, Humalog, Egyptian biosimilars — and counsel on storage and injection technique
6. Counsel on **glucose monitoring, foot care and annual screening**, combining medicines, monitoring and lifestyle in one conversation
