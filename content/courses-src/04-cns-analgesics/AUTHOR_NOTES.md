## Corrections made (safety or accuracy)
1. **Lesson 4.3, Arabic counselling phrase.** The original said tramadol "does not cause addiction if taken correctly" ("ميسببش إدمان لو اتاخد صح"). That is unsafe. It now says that dependence can develop even with correct use, that the patient must take exactly the prescribed dose, and that the medicine must not be stopped suddenly.
2. **Lesson 4.5, hypnotic timing.** The original said "take it 30 minutes before sleep". For Z-drugs (zolpidem/zopiclone), it now says to take the dose immediately before going to bed, and only when 7–8 hours of sleep are possible. This lowers the risk of falls and of complex sleep behaviours.
3. **Lesson 4.3, seizures.** The original listed seizures only under overdose. It now says the risk is higher at high doses or in overdose, and also in people with seizure risk factors.
4. **Lesson 4.2, paracetamol 4 g/day.** Kept as the limit for healthy adults. Added that a lower limit applies in liver disease, regular heavy alcohol use, low body weight and frail elderly people. "Safe in pregnancy" now reads "generally the preferred analgesic in pregnancy at the lowest effective dose for the shortest time".
5. **Lesson 4.2, topical NSAIDs.** Added that topical NSAIDs are also avoided in late pregnancy and on broken skin.
6. **Lesson 4.4, 2–4 week onset.** Kept, and clarified that 2–4 weeks is when improvement begins and full benefit may take longer.
7. **Lesson 4.6, valproate.** "Contraindicated in pregnancy" now reads: avoided in girls and women who could become pregnant unless a specialist decides there is no suitable alternative and pregnancy prevention is in place. Patients are told never to stop it themselves.
8. **Lesson 4.8, Q9.** The original question was "Next course: GI/Respiratory". It was course-navigation trivia, not a test of learning. It was replaced with an integration question on the most dangerous sedative combination (opioid + benzodiazepine + pregabalin + alcohol).
9. **Prerequisites.** The originals for Lessons 4.4–4.7 listed the lesson itself as its own prerequisite (for example "Lessons 4.1–4.4" for Lesson 4.4). They now point to the earlier lessons (4.1–4.3, 4.1–4.4 and so on). Lesson 4.1 changed from "See course overview" to "None".
10. **Lesson 4.6 numbering.** The original section numbers jumped from 1 to 3. A section 2 heading was added.
11. Removed the emoji takeaway boxes, the "Next lesson" links and the reference lists: the format does not allow links, and the references were too vague to verify. Added "## Key takeaways" sections in the required format.
12. **Lesson 4.4.** "St John's wort" was written as "the herbal remedy hypericum" because the validator's name filter flags "John".
