## Course description

This course is for pharmacists and pharmacy staff working in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt who serve women at every life stage: contraception, pregnancy, breastfeeding, menopause and pregnancy planning. Women and their families often ask these questions quietly at the counter, by phone or on WhatsApp, and frequently on behalf of someone else. The quality and safety of the answer depend on the pharmacist asking the right questions, checking the leaflet and current references, and knowing when to contact the prescriber or refer to a doctor.

The course covers contraceptive options available in the Egyptian market, medicine safety in pregnancy, medicine compatibility with breastfeeding, menopause and hormone replacement therapy (HRT) products, and fertility medicines and prenatal supplements. Each lesson combines product knowledge with counselling scripts in English and Arabic, red flags that need referral, privacy and sensitivity at the counter, and a realistic scenario from an Egyptian branch.

Each lesson takes about 90 minutes and ends with a 10-question quiz; a 20-question scenario-based final exam covers the whole course. Pregnancy and breastfeeding safety is an area where caution comes first: when in doubt, check the leaflet and an up-to-date reference and speak to the prescriber. Pharmacists counsel and refer; they do not diagnose. Always follow the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority and the Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

## Learning outcomes

1. List the contraceptive options available in Egypt — pills, IUD, implant, injectable and emergency contraception — and counsel on correct use, missed pills and interactions
2. Apply a structured pregnancy risk assessment to common prescriptions and OTC requests, including medicines that must be avoided
3. Counsel breastfeeding mothers on compatible medicines and safer alternatives, and on when to check a lactation reference
4. Identify HRT products in the Egyptian market and explain their benefits, risks and contraindications in plain language
5. Recommend appropriate prenatal supplements, especially folic acid, and support women using fertility treatments under specialist care
6. Recognise red flags in women's health and refer promptly while protecting the customer's privacy and dignity
