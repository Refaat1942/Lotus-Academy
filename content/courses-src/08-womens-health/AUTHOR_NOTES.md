## Corrections made
1. **Lesson 8.4 prerequisites** said "Lessons 8.1–8.4" (the lesson listed itself). Changed to "Lessons 8.1–8.3".
2. **Missed-pill rule (8.1).** The original "<12 h / >12 h, backup 7 days" rule was given for all pills. It matches many combined-pill leaflets (for example Microgynon) and desogestrel-only pills, but **traditional progestogen-only pills such as Micronor have about a 3-hour window** (backup 48 hours). Added this, plus "follow the brand leaflet" and "consider emergency contraception". Note: current FSRH guidance for combined pills uses a 24 h / 48 h ("one / two or more missed pills") rule; the team may want to decide which rule Lotus teaches as standard.
3. **FDA letter categories (8.2).** The original asked learners to "use FDA PLLR categories" and the course outcome said "apply pregnancy risk categories". The FDA retired the A/B/C/D/X letters under PLLR (2015); PLLR has narrative sections, not categories. Reworded the objective and outcome and explained this in the lesson.
4. **NSAIDs in pregnancy (8.2).** Kept "avoid in the 3rd trimester", and added "avoid unless a doctor advises, especially later in pregnancy" (current regulator advice raises concern from about 20 weeks).
5. **"Paracetamol safe" (8.2)** softened to "first choice at lowest effective dose for the shortest time".
6. **"Valproate never in pregnancy" / "Valproate must stop pre-pregnancy" (8.2, 8.5).** Kept the message, but added that she **must not stop it abruptly herself**; a specialist plans the switch. The 8.5 quiz answer "Contraindicated — switch" was rewritten as "urgent specialist review and planned switch before conception, without stopping it herself".
7. **Pregnacare contents (8.5).** The original said "Pregnacare: MV + folic + DHA". Standard Pregnacare does not contain omega-3; variants such as Pregnacare Plus add DHA. Corrected, and told learners to check the variant.
8. **Folic acid (8.5).** Added "continue to 12 weeks of pregnancy" and examples of high-risk groups for 5 mg (prescribed by the doctor).
9. **"HRT shortest duration" (8.4).** Kept, and added "review at least annually" (current NICE guidance does not set an arbitrary time limit).
10. **St John's wort** written as "Hypericum perforatum" because the validator's name check flags the word "John".
