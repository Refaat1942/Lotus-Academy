## 1. Heart failure pharmacotherapy

### 1.1 HFrEF — four pillars of GDMT (ESC 2021/2023)

| Pillar | Drug class | Egyptian examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ACE-I / ARB / ARNI | Tritace, Tareg, Entresto (limited) |
| 2 | Beta-blocker | Concor, Dilatrend, Betaloc ZOK |
| 3 | MRA (mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist) | Aldactone (spironolactone) |
| 4 | SGLT2 inhibitor | Forxiga (dapagliflozin), Jardiance (empagliflozin) |

**Evidence:** PARADIGM-HF (sacubitril/valsartan), DAPA-HF and EMPEROR-Reduced (SGLT2 inhibitors) — reductions in mortality and hospitalization. Other options such as ivabradine are added by the cardiologist in selected patients.

### 1.2 Diuretics in HF

| Drug | Brand (Egypt) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Furosemide | Lasix, EIPICO generic | Symptom relief — congestion |
| Torasemide | Demadex | Alternative loop diuretic |
| Spironolactone | Aldactone | Mortality benefit (RALES) + diuresis |
| Metolazone | Limited | Add-on for diuretic resistance |

### 1.3 Digoxin — Egyptian market

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Lanoxin, Digoxin (Memphis, EIPICO) |
| **Mechanism** | Na/K-ATPase inhibition → ↑ contractility; ↑ vagal tone → ↓ heart rate in AF |
| **Dosing** | 0.125–0.25 mg OD; adjust for renal function and age |
| **Level** | Therapeutic 0.5–1.0 ng/mL (narrow therapeutic index) |
| **Approx. cost** | 40–100 EGP/month |

**Digoxin toxicity:** nausea, vomiting, loss of appetite, visual changes (yellow-green tint or halos), arrhythmias — any new arrhythmia in a patient on digoxin is toxicity until proven otherwise. Hypokalemia increases the risk of toxicity.

**Contraindications:** WPW syndrome with AF, hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy, complete heart block.

## 2. Antiarrhythmics

### 2.1 Vaughan Williams classification

| Class | Mechanism | Egyptian examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Ia** | Na⁺ channel block (moderate) | Disopyramide (limited) |
| **Ib** | Na⁺ channel block (short) | Lidocaine (IV) |
| **Ic** | Na⁺ channel block (strong) | Propafenone (Rythmol — limited) |
| **II** | Beta-blockade | Concor, Inderal |
| **III** | K⁺ channel block | Cordarone (amiodarone), sotalol |
| **IV** | Ca²⁺ channel block | Verapamil, diltiazem |

### 2.2 Amiodarone — Egyptian market

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Cordarone (Sanofi), Amiodarone (Pharco, Memphis) |
| **Indications** | AF/flutter, VT/VF (refractory), rate/rhythm control |
| **Loading** | 200 mg TDS × 1 week → 200 mg BD × 1 week → 200 mg OD maintenance |
| **Half-life** | 40–60 days — effects persist for months after stopping |
| **Monitoring** | Thyroid function (hypo/hyperthyroidism), liver function, lungs (pulmonary fibrosis), eyes (corneal deposits) |
| **Interactions** | ↑ warfarin INR significantly; ↑ digoxin levels (↑ toxicity) |
| **Approx. cost** | 80–200 EGP/month |

### 2.3 Rate control in AF

| Agent | Brand (Egypt) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Metoprolol | Lopressor, Betaloc | First-line rate control |
| Bisoprolol | Concor | HF + AF |
| Diltiazem | Dilzem (limited) | Avoid in HFrEF |
| Digoxin | Lanoxin | Elderly, sedentary patients; poor control during exercise |
| Amiodarone | Cordarone | Rhythm + rate; reserved for refractory cases |

**Counseling script (Arabic):**

> "قصور القلب محتاج التزام بكل الأدوية — مثبط ACE، والكونكور، والألداكتون، وممكن الفورزيجا. متوقفش مدر البول لو حسيت إنك أحسن — الدكتور هو اللي ينظم الجرعة. لو بتاخد لانوكسين: لو حسيت بغثيان، أو شفت الألوان متغيرة، أو حسيت بخفقان غريب، روح الطوارئ. الكوردارون محتاج تحليل غدة وكبد كل 6 شهور."

*English meaning:* heart failure needs commitment to all the medicines — the ACE inhibitor, Concor, Aldactone and possibly Forxiga. Do not stop the diuretic because you feel better — the doctor adjusts the dose. If you take Lanoxin: nausea, changed colour vision or unusual palpitations mean go to the emergency department. Cordarone needs thyroid and liver tests every 6 months.

## 3. Contraindications & interactions summary

| Drug | Critical interaction |
|---|---|
| Digoxin | Amiodarone ↑ digoxin levels by about 70% — reduce the digoxin dose by about 50% |
| Amiodarone | Warfarin — reduce the warfarin dose by about 30–50% when starting, with close INR checks |
| Beta-blocker + verapamil | Severe bradycardia |
| SGLT2 inhibitor | DKA risk if insulin is stopped abruptly; genital infections |
| NSAIDs | Fluid retention — worsen HF |

## 4. Pharmacy practice scenario

### Scenario 1.7 — HF patient wants to stop the diuretic

> *A customer with HFrEF takes Tritace + Concor + Aldactone + Lasix 40 mg. He feels well and wants to stop Lasix to reduce bathroom trips.*

**Response:**

1. Explain that the diuretic treats congestion symptoms, while the GDMT drugs (ACE-I, beta-blocker, MRA, SGLT2 inhibitor) protect survival
2. Do NOT advise stopping without the physician — risk of fluid overload
3. Suggest a physician visit — the doctor may reduce the dose (for example to 20 mg) or adjust timing
4. Counsel daily weight monitoring — a gain of more than 2 kg in 3 days → call the physician
5. Reinforce salt-reduction counseling — the Egyptian diet is often high in sodium

## Key takeaways

- HFrEF GDMT has four pillars: ACE-I/ARB/ARNI + beta-blocker + MRA + SGLT2 inhibitor
- Lasix (furosemide) gives symptom relief; Aldactone (spironolactone) gives a mortality benefit
- Lanoxin (digoxin) has a narrow therapeutic index; toxicity is worsened by hypokalemia and amiodarone
- Cordarone (amiodarone) is the most used class III drug in Egypt; monitor thyroid, liver, lungs and eyes
- Amiodarone dramatically increases the effect of warfarin — a common Egyptian interaction
- Forxiga/Jardiance are now part of HF therapy regardless of diabetes status
- Rapid weight gain, worsening swelling or breathlessness means same-day medical review
