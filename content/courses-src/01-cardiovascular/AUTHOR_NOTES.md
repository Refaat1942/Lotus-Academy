## Clinical / factual corrections

1. **Tareg = valsartan, not losartan.** The source listed Tareg (and Co-Tareg) as losartan by MSD. In Egypt Tareg is Novartis' valsartan brand and Co-Tareg is valsartan + HCTZ. Corrected in Lessons 1.1, 1.2, 1.4, 1.7, 1.8 and in the quizzes:
   - L1.1 Q9 "Tareg contains" — answer changed from losartan to **valsartan**.
   - L1.2 Q2 "Tareg contains" — answer changed from losartan to **valsartan**.
   - L1.4 Q3 "Co-Tareg contains" — answer changed from losartan + HCTZ to **valsartan + HCTZ**.
   - Losartan brand now given as Cozaar (MSD) plus local generics. The source names "Lsartan" and "Losar" (Pharco/Memphis) could not be confirmed and were replaced by "local generics" — please re-add the exact local brand names if they are real.
   - Case 1 (L1.8) "Tareg generic" changed to "a losartan or valsartan generic".
2. **"Liptior" corrected to "Lipitor"** (Pfizer atorvastatin) everywhere, including L1.5 Q3.
3. **ACE-cough counseling script (L1.2):** the source told patients the doctor could switch them to "another drug from the same group (like Tareg)". ARBs are a different class; the script now says "a related group (ARB such as losartan or valsartan)" and adds the angioedema warning.
4. **ARBs and dialysis (L1.2 renal table):** the source said losartan/telmisartan are "dialyzable". They are not removed by dialysis; several ACE inhibitors (captopril, enalapril, lisinopril) are. Row rewritten as "specialist dosing".
5. **Warfarin counseling (L1.6):** "orange urine" listed as a bleeding sign was changed to "red or dark urine, black stools" (warfarin itself can colour urine orange harmlessly; blood in urine is red/dark).
6. **Ciprofloxacin + warfarin (L1.8 Case 3):** "may increase or decrease INR unpredictably" changed to "usually increases the INR (effect varies)".
7. **Co-Amlessa (L1.4, L1.8):** listed as perindopril + amlodipine (Servier). As far as we know Amlessa is perindopril + amlodipine and Co-Amlessa adds indapamide (triple), and it is not a Servier product. The table now reads "Amlessa / Co-Amlessa — perindopril + amlodipine (Co-Amlessa adds indapamide) — generic". In the L1.8 step-2 algorithm Co-Amlessa was replaced by Coveram. Please confirm the local manufacturer.
8. **Co-Tareg Forte (L1.4):** source gave "losartan 100 mg + HCTZ 25 mg". Replaced with "Co-Tareg (higher strengths) — valsartan 160 mg + HCTZ"; please confirm the exact strengths marketed in Egypt.
9. **Lasix wording (L1.4 takeaways):** "Lasix from EIPICO" changed to "Lasix (Sanofi) plus EIPICO generic furosemide", matching the lesson's own table.
10. **NSAIDs in L1.8 takeaways:** "the #1 hidden cause" softened to "a leading hidden cause" (no source for the ranking). "Generic substitution saves patients 50–70%" softened to "can substantially reduce patients' costs" (unsourced figure).
11. **Statin muscle-pain scenario (L1.5):** added the red flag "severe pain or dark urine → stop and seek care immediately" so the "do not stop without the physician" advice cannot be misapplied.
12. **First-line algorithm (L1.1):** "all 4 classes equal per NICE/ESC" removed (NICE does not treat them as equal); beta-blocker kept as "not first line unless specific indication", as in the source. The "JNC 8 meta-analysis" attribution for the weight-loss figure was removed (not a JNC 8 output); the figure itself was kept.
13. **St John's wort** renamed "Hypericum (herbal antidepressant)" only because the platform validator flags the word "John" as a personal name.
