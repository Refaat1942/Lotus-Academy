## 1. Lipid physiology & targets

### 1.1 Atherosclerosis mechanism

- ↑ LDL-C → deposition in the arterial wall (intima) → foam cells → plaque → cardiovascular events
- **Statins:** inhibit HMG-CoA reductase → ↓ hepatic cholesterol synthesis → ↑ LDL receptors on liver cells → LDL-C falls by 30–50%
- Beyond LDL lowering, statins have "pleiotropic" effects such as plaque stabilisation and reduced vascular inflammation

**Evidence:** CTT Collaboration meta-analysis — each 1 mmol/L LDL reduction → 22% relative reduction in major vascular events. The WOSCOPS, 4S and LIPID trials established the mortality benefit.

### 1.2 Lipid targets (ESC/EAS 2019–2021 / Egyptian practice)

| Risk category | LDL-C target |
|---|---|
| Very high risk (established ASCVD, diabetes with target-organ damage) | **<55 mg/dL** (<1.4 mmol/L) |
| High risk (diabetes, CKD, familial hypercholesterolemia) | **<70 mg/dL** (<1.8 mmol/L) |
| Moderate risk | **<100 mg/dL** (<2.6 mmol/L) |
| Low risk | **<116 mg/dL** (<3.0 mmol/L) |

**Egyptian context:** dyslipidemia affects about 40% of adults; statin adherence is low because of cost and fear of muscle symptoms.

### 2.1 Potency comparison

| Statin | LDL reduction at starting dose |
|---|---|
| Rosuvastatin 10 mg | about 45–50% |
| Atorvastatin 20 mg | about 40–45% |
| Simvastatin 40 mg | about 35–40% |
| Pravastatin 40 mg | about 30–35% |

### 2.2 Contraindications & interactions

| Contraindication | Interaction |
|---|---|
| Active liver disease, pregnancy (and breastfeeding) | Gemfibrozil + simvastatin → rhabdomyolysis |
| Unexplained persistent ↑ transaminases | Amlodipine → limit simvastatin to 20 mg |
| | Clarithromycin, erythromycin, azole antifungals → ↑ statin levels |
| | Grapefruit juice → ↑ simvastatin/atorvastatin levels |

**Counseling script (Arabic):**

> "الستاتين بيتاخد بالليل أو في أي وقت ثابت يومياً. ممكن تحس بألم عضلات خفيف في الأول — لو الألم شديد أو معاه بول غامق، وقف الدواء وروح للدكتور فوراً. متشربش جريب فروت مع السيمفاستاتين. الكوليسترول محتاج 6–8 أسابيع عشان تشوف النتيجة في التحليل."

*English meaning:* take the statin at night or at any fixed time every day. You may feel mild muscle aches at first — if the pain is severe or comes with dark urine, stop the medicine and see the doctor immediately. Do not drink grapefruit juice with simvastatin. It takes 6–8 weeks to see the result in the cholesterol test.

## 4. Pharmacy practice scenario

### Scenario 1.5 — Statin muscle pain

> *A customer on atorvastatin 40 mg (Ator, Amoun) for 2 months reports aching in both thighs. He is also taking erythromycin for a respiratory infection.*

**Response:**

1. Recognize statin-associated muscle symptoms (SAMS) plus a macrolide interaction that raises statin levels
2. Advise prompt physician contact — the doctor may check CK, especially if pain is severe or urine is dark
3. Counsel: do not stop on your own without speaking to the doctor, but make sure the doctor knows about the erythromycin (if pain is severe or urine is dark, stop and seek care immediately)
4. The physician may pause the statin during the antibiotic, reduce the dose or switch (for example to pravastatin)
5. Do not dispense additional NSAID painkillers without considering kidney function and BP

## Key takeaways

- Atorvastatin (Lipitor/Ator) and rosuvastatin (Crestor) dominate the Egyptian market
- Amoun, Pharco and Memphis produce affordable atorvastatin/rosuvastatin generics
- LDL target is <55 mg/dL for very-high-risk patients with established ASCVD
- Simvastatin maximum 20 mg with amlodipine — a common Egyptian HTN + dyslipidemia combination
- SAMS management: rule out interactions (macrolides, azoles, gemfibrozil), then a lower dose or alternative statin
- Ezetimibe (Ezetrol) is available as an add-on when LDL is not at target
- Severe muscle pain with dark urine is a red flag — stop and seek medical care immediately
