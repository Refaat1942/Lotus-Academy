## 1. Diuretic mechanisms

### 1.1 Classification

| Class | Site | Examples (Egypt) | Key effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thiazide / thiazide-like** | Distal convoluted tubule (DCT) | HCTZ, indapamide (Natrilix) | Na⁺/Cl⁻ excretion; ↓ BP |
| **Loop** | Thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle | Furosemide (Lasix), torasemide | Potent diuresis |
| **K-sparing** | Collecting duct | Spironolactone (Aldactone), amiloride | ↓ K⁺ loss; anti-aldosterone |

- **Thiazides:** initial ↓ plasma volume → long-term ↓ peripheral resistance
- **Loop diuretics:** inhibit the Na-K-2Cl cotransporter → rapid diuresis
- **Spironolactone:** aldosterone antagonist → mortality benefit in HFrEF (RALES trial)

**Evidence:** ALLHAT — chlorthalidone equivalent to amlodipine. RALES — spironolactone reduced HF mortality by about 30%.

### 1.4 Contraindications & interactions

| Diuretic | Contraindications | Key interactions |
|---|---|---|
| Thiazides | Anuria, severe renal impairment (eGFR <30) | ↑ lithium toxicity; ↓ hypoglycemic effect of sulfonylureas |
| Loop | Anuria, severe hypokalemia | Ototoxicity with aminoglycosides |
| Spironolactone | Hyperkalemia, Addison's disease | ACE-I/ARB → dangerous hyperkalemia; avoid K+ supplements |
| All | Gout (thiazides ↑ uric acid) | NSAIDs ↓ diuretic efficacy |

**Counseling script (Arabic):**

> "مدر البول بيزود التبول — خده الصبح عشان متتعبش بالليل. لو حسيت بدوخة، قوم ببطء. راقب علامات نقص البوتاسيوم: تقلصات وتعب في العضلات، ضعف، عدم انتظام ضربات القلب. متاخدش مكملات بوتاسيوم من غير تحليل واستشارة دكتور."

*English meaning:* the diuretic increases urination — take it in the morning so it does not disturb your night. If you feel dizzy, stand up slowly. Watch for signs of low potassium: muscle cramps and fatigue, weakness, irregular heartbeat. Do not take potassium supplements without a blood test and a doctor's advice.

### 2.2 Advantages of FDC in Egyptian practice

- **Adherence:** one pill instead of two — critical in Egypt, where polypharmacy is common
- **Cost:** Egyptian generics are often cheaper than two separate branded products
- **Step therapy:** matches the ESC/NICE algorithm directly

## 3. Pharmacy practice scenario

### Scenario 1.4 — Hypokalemia on HCTZ

> *A customer has been on Co-Tareg (valsartan/HCTZ) for 6 months. Complains of muscle cramps and fatigue. Takes ibuprofen (Brufen) daily for knee pain.*

**Response:**

1. Recognize the hypokalemia risk from HCTZ, plus the NSAID interaction
2. Advise a physician review — serum K+ and creatinine are needed
3. Counsel stopping unsupervised daily ibuprofen — it worsens BP control and kidney function
4. Do NOT sell KCl supplements without a prescription and a K+ level
5. Explain that the ARB component (valsartan) partially protects against thiazide hypokalemia — so the cause must be confirmed by a blood test

## Key takeaways

- HCTZ and indapamide (Natrilix) are key thiazide-type agents in Egypt — very affordable
- Furosemide (Lasix, plus EIPICO generic furosemide) is the standard loop diuretic
- Spironolactone (Aldactone) has a mortality benefit in HF; watch K+ when combined with an ACE-I/ARB
- Exforge, Co-Tareg and Co-Diovan are major branded FDCs; Co-Tareg and Co-Diovan contain **valsartan + HCTZ**
- Triple therapy (ARB/ACE-I + CCB + diuretic) is used for HTN not controlled on two drugs
- NSAIDs antagonize diuretics and raise BP — a common Egyptian interaction
- Single-pill combinations improve adherence and are often cheaper as local generics
