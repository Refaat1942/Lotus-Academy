## Course description

GI acid suppression, antiemetics, IBS, respiratory inhalers, and cold/flu products in the Egyptian market. This course is for pharmacists and pharmacy staff in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt who handle some of the most frequent requests at the counter: heartburn and stomach pain, nausea and bloating, diarrhoea in adults and children, "liver tonics", asthma and COPD inhalers, allergy and the common cold.

Each lesson explains how the main medicine classes work, links generic names to the brands customers ask for in Egypt, and turns that knowledge into practical counselling: when and how to take a medicine, which risks to explain, how to teach inhaler technique, and which red flags mean the customer must see a doctor. Every lesson includes ready-to-use Arabic counselling phrases and a realistic scenario from a busy Lotus branch.

The course has six lessons of about 90 minutes, each ending with a 10-question quiz, and a 20-question scenario-based final exam. Pharmacists counsel and refer; they do not diagnose. Always follow the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority and the Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP, especially for prescription-only medicines.

## Learning outcomes

1. Compare **PPI vs H2** — Nexium, Controloc, Antodine — and counsel on timing, long-term risks and H. pylori therapy
2. Manage **IBS and antiemetics** with Egyptian brands, including the cardiac and neurological risks of prokinetics
3. Counsel safely on acute diarrhoea, giving ORS priority in children and using antidiarrheals and probiotics appropriately
4. Advise on **hepatoprotectants** — evidence and counseling — and on gallstone medicines and paracetamol safety
5. Counsel **inhaler technique** — Ventolin, Seretide, Symbicort — and explain rescue versus controller treatment
6. Guide **OTC cold/flu** selection — Telfast, Otrivin, Egyptian products — with attention to sedation and blood pressure
