## Corrections made
1. **Lesson 5.1 — Antodine:** the source listed "Cimetidine (Antodine)". Antodine is a famotidine brand in Egypt; corrected to famotidine (Pepcid, Antodine). Cimetidine is now mentioned only as an older H2 blocker with more interactions. Quiz answer "Antodine = H2 blocker" was already correct.
2. **Lesson 5.1 — H. pylori:** kept "PPI + amoxicillin + clarithromycin (Klacid) × 14 days" as a common Egyptian regimen, but added that clarithromycin resistance is a concern and doctors may prescribe other regimens; staff dispense what is prescribed. Q5 rewritten from "Triple therapy" to "a prescribed combination (triple therapy) of a PPI plus two antibiotics, completed in full".
3. **Lesson 5.1 — PPI timing:** clarified "before the first meal of the day, usually breakfast"; added red flags for referral.
4. **Lesson 5.2 — Domperidone "QT monitoring":** pharmacists cannot monitor QT. Reworded to screening for QT risk (age, heart disease, interacting drugs such as some macrolides/azoles) and referral; short courses usually no more than one week.
5. **Lesson 5.3 — Probiotics:** "Probiotics with antibiotics help" softened to "can help reduce the risk of antibiotic-associated diarrhoea; evidence varies by strain", with a caution for severely ill or immunocompromised patients.
6. **Lesson 5.3 — Loperamide:** added warning not to exceed the label dose (cardiac toxicity at high doses) and not to use with blood/fever.
7. **Lesson 5.4 — NASH:** kept the term and added the current name MASLD/MASH.
8. **Lesson 5.5 — Inhaler technique:** the source described only MDI technique. Added dry powder inhaler technique (do not shake, fast deep breath), since Symbicort Turbuhaler and Seretide Diskus are common. Added that current international asthma guidance no longer supports reliever-only (SABA-only) treatment in adults/adolescents and that Symbicort may be prescribed as maintenance and reliever.
9. **Lesson 5.6 — Phenylephrine:** added that regulators in some countries have questioned the effectiveness of oral phenylephrine; added a caution against cough/cold combinations in young children without a doctor's advice.
10. **Format:** removed the reference lists and "Next lesson" links (links are not allowed); converted the emoji blockquote takeaways into "## Key takeaways" bullet lists. Original references were: ACG GERD guidelines, Nexium PI, WHO H. pylori guidelines, Takeda Controloc PI; EMA domperidone guidance, NICE IBS, Sanofi Motilium PI, ACG nausea guidelines; WHO diarrhoea guidelines, EMA loperamide guidance, Egyptian Pediatric Society, Normix PI; EASL liver guidelines, AASLD HBV guidelines, Ursofalk PI, WHO viral hepatitis; GINA, GOLD, NHS inhaler technique, GSK Ventolin PI; ARIA, BNF nasal decongestants, FDA OTC cold guidance, GSK Otrivin PI.
