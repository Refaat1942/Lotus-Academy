## Course description

This course is for pharmacists, technicians, cashiers, delivery staff and branch managers working in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt. It treats service as part of care: every customer who comes to the branch, calls, or writes on WhatsApp is judged on the whole experience, from the first greeting to the follow-up after the sale.

You will learn how to see the visit as a journey with moments that matter, how to set the standards of welcome, appearance, counter etiquette and queue management, and how to handle unavailable stock honestly. You will practise a simple framework for complaints and service recovery, serve customers by phone, WhatsApp and delivery with courtesy and privacy, and use feedback, satisfaction score and Net Promoter Score to improve the branch week by week.

The course has five lessons of about 80 to 90 minutes. Each lesson has scripts in English and Arabic, checklists, common mistakes, an Egyptian-pharmacy scenario, a short video script for the team, and a ten-question quiz. A final exam of twenty questions covers all lessons.

## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the customer journey in a Lotus pharmacy and identify the moments that matter.
2. Apply Lotus standards for welcome, appearance, counter etiquette and busy-hour queue management.
3. Handle unavailable stock honestly and offer a useful next step with the customer's consent.
4. Resolve complaints with the Listen-Apologise-Solve-Thank framework, acting within empowerment limits and escalating safety concerns.
5. Provide professional phone, WhatsApp, delivery and online-order service that protects customer privacy.
6. Collect feedback and interpret a satisfaction score, a Net Promoter Score and customer comments.
7. Run a weekly team routine that closes the loop with customers and builds loyalty.
