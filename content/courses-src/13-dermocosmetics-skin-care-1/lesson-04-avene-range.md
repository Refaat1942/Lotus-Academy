## Learning objectives

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

1. Group the Avène products in the Lotus list by customer need: dry skin, oily skin and acne, cleansing, sun protection, anti-wrinkle, soothing and healing.
2. State what each Avène product on the list is for, using only the Lotus usage notes.
3. Choose the right Avène sunscreen for dry, oily, normal and sensitive skin, for children and after procedures.
4. Counsel customers with very dry, cracked or sensitive skin with clear and safe language.
5. Explain moderate and advanced acne products and know when to send the customer to a dermatologist.
6. Identify the items whose sheet entries are missing or unclear and ask the Lotus team instead of guessing.

## 1. Reading the Avène sheet

The Lotus Avène sheet is a list of products with short Arabic usage notes. It is our only authority for what each product is for. Several entries have no usage note, or contain wording that looks like a typo. These are marked **(verify)**. Say only what the sheet says, and never invent ingredients, percentages or indications.

Pharmacist's role reminder: we counsel on cosmetic care; for acne in moderate or advanced stages, eczema-like rashes, burns or wounds, the doctor decides.

## 2. Dry and very dry skin

| Product | What the Lotus sheet says | Who it suits and what to say |
|---|---|---|
| **Akerat Cream 200 ml** | Moisturiser for very dry skin that suffers from scales | Very dry, scaly skin on the body. If the scaling is thick, cracked, painful or does not improve, see a doctor. |
| **Cold Cream 100 ml** | Strong moisturiser for dry skin | Dry skin that needs a rich cream. |
| **Cold Cream For Hands** | Strong moisturiser for cracked hands and heels | Hands exposed to washing, heels with cracks. Apply after each hand wash. |
| **Rich Compensating Cream 40 ml** | Daily moisturiser for dry and very dry skin | A daily face cream for dry skin. |
| **Trixera Cream** | Moisturiser for dry skin that is prone to allergy (sensitivity) | Dry skin that reacts easily. Patch test first. |
| **Skin Recovery cream** | No usage note in the sheet | **(verify)** Do not describe. |

**Counselling tips**

- Cream right after a short lukewarm shower.
- A rich cream on hands after each wash is more useful than a large amount once a day.
- Deep cracks that bleed or look infected go to a doctor.

## 3. Oily skin and acne

| Product | What the Lotus sheet says | Who it suits and what to say |
|---|---|---|
| **Cleanance gel 200 ml** | Daily wash for oily skin | The first step for oily or shiny skin. |
| **Clean AC 40 ml** | Listed as a moisturiser for oily skin (the sheet adds "the only moisturiser", which we do not repeat; verify the name and wording) | Oily skin that needs a light moisturiser. |
| **Cleanance K** | Exfoliator for blackheads | Oily skin with blackheads. Use as the pack directs and avoid inflamed or irritated skin. |
| **Diacneal cream 30 ml** | Cream for all types of acne in the moderate and advanced stages (as worded in the sheet) | Acne that is not mild. Moderate or advanced acne should be seen by a dermatologist; the cream supports a doctor's plan and does not replace one. |

**Counselling tips**

- Wash twice a day, no scrubbing.
- Do not combine several treatments without advice; this often irritates.
- Judge results after several weeks, not days.
- Painful, deep, scarring, widespread or suddenly appearing adult acne goes to a dermatologist.

## Scenario

A mother in a busy branch wants a sunscreen for her six-year-old and for her husband who works outdoors, and a cream for the dry, cracked heels of her mother-in-law. The pharmacist chooses from the list the sunscreen noted for children and sensitive skin, and a sunscreen for dry or normal-to-oily skin depending on the husband, then the cold cream listed for cracked hands and heels. The pharmacist explains re-application, shade and clothing, and that if the heel cracks bleed or look infected the family should see a doctor.

## Key takeaways

- Avène is easiest to learn by need: dry, oily and acne, cleansing, sun, anti-wrinkle, soothing and healing.
- Akerat Cream is for very dry, scaly skin; Cold Cream for hands and heels is for cracks.
- Sunscreen choice: dry skin cream, oily and normal emulsion, sensitive skin and children Extreme SPF 50, tinted for colour.
- After laser or peeling, the doctor's advice comes first.
- Diacneal cream is listed for moderate and advanced acne: this is a dermatologist case.
- Items without a usage note in the sheet are not described to customers until verified.
- Honest results: weeks for acne, months for wrinkles and pigmentation.
