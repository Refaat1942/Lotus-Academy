## Course description

This course is for pharmacists and pharmacy staff in Lotus pharmacies in Egypt who advise walk-in, phone and WhatsApp customers on skin care. Customers arrive with a product name from social media or a friend; this course teaches you to start from the skin and the need, to ask the right safety questions, and to recommend a suitable product without exaggerating.

The first lesson builds the foundation: skin types, the consultation, red flags that require a doctor, and sun-protection and patch-test basics. The next three lessons cover the Bioderma, Uriage and Avène products on the Lotus list, using the Lotus product sheet as the only source for what each product is for. The final lesson compares the three ranges by need, shows how to build a simple morning and evening routine, and covers expectations, ethical add-ons and referral.

Each lesson has tables, model customer phrases, a scenario and a 10-question quiz, and each has a video script for recording. The course ends with a 20-question final exam. Pharmacists counsel and do not diagnose: wherever a condition may be medical, the course tells you when to refer to a doctor or dermatologist.

## Learning outcomes

1. Identify dry, normal, oily, combination and sensitive skin through short consultation questions.
2. Collect safety information about pregnancy, breastfeeding, children, allergies and earlier reactions before recommending a product.
3. Recognise red flags that require a doctor instead of a cosmetic product.
4. Explain what each Bioderma, Uriage and Avène product on the Lotus list is for, without adding claims.
5. Choose suitable sunscreens for different skin types, children, pregnancy and breastfeeding, and after procedures.
6. Build and explain a simple morning and evening routine with realistic expectations.
7. Offer ethical add-ons and refer customers at the right moment.
