## Learning objectives

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

1. Name the Bioderma lines in the Lotus list: dry skin (Atoderm), sun (Photoderm), sensitive skin (Sensebio), oily skin (Sebium), healing and hair-scalp.
2. State what each Bioderma product on the Lotus list is for, using the Lotus usage notes only.
3. Match a customer's skin type and need to the right Bioderma line and texture.
4. Explain each product to the customer in short, honest sentences without adding claims.
5. Recognise unclear items and refer to the pack or the Lotus team instead of guessing.
6. Decide when a customer should see a doctor instead.

## 1. How to use this lesson

The tables below are built from the **Lotus product sheet**. The sheet is the only source for what each product is for. If a customer asks about an ingredient, a percentage, an age limit or a medical effect that is not in these tables, read the pack or the leaflet, ask the Lotus team, and never improvise. A few items have unclear wording in the sheet; they are marked **(verify)** and the Lotus team is checking them.

Bioderma is organised by skin need. The line names are easy to remember:

| Line | Skin need |
|---|---|
| **Atoderm** | Dry skin |
| **Photoderm** | Sun protection (and related care listed in the sheet) |
| **Sensebio** | Sensitive skin |
| **Sebium** | Oily skin |
| **Cicabio** (written "Ciacbio" in the sheet, verify) | Healing |
| **Node Ds+** | Hair and scalp |

## 2. Dry skin: Atoderm

| Product | What the Lotus sheet says | Who it suits and what to say |
|---|---|---|
| **Atoderm Cream** | Daily moisturiser for dry and normal skin, face and body, "night"; does not block pores | The everyday cream. "It moisturises dry and normal skin on the face and body and is described as not blocking pores." |
| **Atoderm Milk** | "Day"; a lotion for dry skin, used on large areas, no greasy feel | Dry skin that dislikes a greasy feel, or large body areas. "A lighter lotion for the whole body." |
| **Atoderm Soap** | Soap for dry skin, with cucumber extract and glycerine | Customer who still prefers a bar. "A soap made for dry skin; moisturise afterwards." |
| **Atoderm Mossant** | Cleansing gel for dry skin, fragrance-free | Dry skin and a customer who dislikes fragrance. "A gel cleanser without perfume." (Name spelled "Mossant" in the sheet.) |
| **Atoderm Po Zinc** | Cream for skin irritation, cortisone-free, for nappy (diaper) rash in babies | A mother asking about nappy rash. "A cortisone-free cream for the nappy area." |
| **Atoderm P.p** | For wrinkles caused by dry skin with advancing age **(verify)** | Mature customer with very dry skin. Say only what the sheet says and check the pack. |

**Counselling tips for dry skin**

- Moisturise right after washing, when the skin is still slightly damp.
- Use lukewarm water and short showers.
- Cream at night and a lighter milk by day is a simple way to use the line.
- For a baby's nappy rash: change nappies often, keep the area clean and dry. If the rash is weeping, spreading, bleeding or comes with fever, send to a doctor.

## 3. Sun care: Photoderm

The sheet lists many Photoderm items. Choose by **skin type, area and colour need**.

| Product | What the Lotus sheet says | Who it suits and what to say |
|---|---|---|
| **Photoderm Cream** | For normal or dry skin | Face of a normal or dry-skinned customer. |
| **Photoderm Fluid** | For oily skin, no colour | Oily or shiny skin that wants a light texture. "A fluid, uncoloured sunscreen." |
| **Photoderm Milk** | A cream for large areas | Body, arms, legs. "Easy to spread on large areas." |
| **Photoderm spray** | For normal skin | Convenient application on normal skin. Spray carefully, avoid the face being sprayed directly, and rub in. |
| **Photoderm Tinted** | Used as a foundation for dark skin | Customer with darker skin who wants colour plus protection. |
| **Photoderm Light Tinted** | Used as a foundation for light skin | Customer with lighter skin who wants colour plus protection. |
| **Photoderm Kids** | For children | Parents of children. Add shade and clothing; follow the pack. |
| **Photoderm Spot** | After peeling and laser | Customer who had a procedure. "Ask the doctor's advice on after-care; this is the item the sheet lists for after peeling and laser." |
| **Photoderm w/o cream** | Cream to lighten dark spots, day **(verify)** | See note below. |
| **Photoderm w/o serum** | Cream to lighten dark spots, night **(verify)** | See note below. |

**Note on the two "w/o" items.** The sheet describes them as lightening dark spots, day and night. Because the line is a sun-care line, the wording may be unclear. Do not explain the difference between them beyond the sheet. Ask the Lotus team and read the pack before recommending.

**Counselling tips for sun care**

- Match texture to skin: fluid for oily, cream for normal or dry, milk or spray for the body.
- Tinted products can replace a foundation for customers who want colour.
- Re-apply after sweating or swimming and combine with a hat and shade.
- For dark spots, explain that fading takes time and that the sun makes spots darker, so daily protection matters. A changing spot goes to a doctor.

**Counselling tips for sensitive skin**

- Keep the routine short: cleanse, moisturise, protect.
- Patch test first and introduce one product at a time.
- Avoid rubbing, very hot water and scrubs.
- Persistent redness, burning or swelling goes to a doctor.

| **Sebium Akn** | Cream for acne, removing blackheads and whiteheads, reduces the look of spots | Customer with mild spots and blackheads. Read the pack. |
| **Sebium Ai** | Speeds the clearing of inflamed pimples, reduces getting pimples, moisturises and soothes | Customer with inflamed pimples. |
| **Sebium pore refiner** | Tightens open pores, reduces shine, improves skin feel | Customer worried about pores and shine. |
| **Sebium Soap** | Soap for sensitive skin **(verify)** | The sheet places it in the oily line but describes it for sensitive skin. Read the pack. |

**Counselling tips for oily skin and acne**

- Wash twice a day, not more; over-washing and scrubbing irritate.
- Oily skin still needs a light moisturiser and a fluid sunscreen.
- Never squeeze spots; improvement takes weeks.
- Painful, deep, scarring or widespread acne goes to a dermatologist.

## Scenario

A young man in his twenties asks for something for "oily skin and blackheads", and says he also uses a sunscreen that makes him shine. The pharmacist asks how often he washes, whether he scrubs, and whether any spots are painful. He washes twice and scrubs daily. The pharmacist suggests a daily oily-skin wash from the Sebium line, using the exfoliating product only twice a week, and a fluid sunscreen from the Photoderm line for oily skin. He explains that results take weeks, that squeezing is a mistake, and that painful deep spots should be shown to a dermatologist.

## Key takeaways

- Bioderma is organised by need: Atoderm dry, Photoderm sun, Sensebio sensitive, Sebium oily.
- Atoderm Cream is the night everyday moisturiser, Atoderm Milk the lighter day lotion for large areas.
- Choose Photoderm by texture and colour: Fluid for oily, Cream for normal or dry, Milk and spray for the body, Tinted for colour, Kids for children.
- Photoderm Spot is the item the sheet lists after peeling and laser.
- Sensebio keeps sensitive-skin routines short and simple.
- Sebium Exfoliating is twice weekly only.
- Never guess for items marked verify; read the pack or ask the Lotus team.
