# Final Exam — Communication Skills for Pharmacists

## Final Exam (20 questions)

**Q1.** A customer says "I need something for my cough" while two people wait behind. What is the most useful first move?
- a) Hand over the best-selling syrup
- b) Stop, face the customer and ask an open question about the cough, who it is for and how long ✓
- c) Tell the customer to read the shelf labels
- d) Ask the customer to come back when the branch is quieter

**Q2.** A worried mother describes her child's night cough. Which response combines reflecting and empathy?
- a) "Children always cough, it is nothing."
- b) "Do you want the syrup or the tablets?"
- c) "Please speak faster, others are waiting."
- d) "So it is worse at night and you are worried about him. Let us check a few things together." ✓

**Q3.** A customer mentions chest pain and breathlessness while asking for an antacid. What is correct?
- a) Stay calm, explain that this needs urgent medical care and follow the SOP ✓
- b) Sell the antacid and explain the dose
- c) Suggest a second product
- d) Ask the customer to return if it continues

**Q4.** Which wording is the best teach-back request?
- a) "Do you understand?"
- b) "Is it clear?"
- c) "To be sure I explained well, how will you give it to your child tomorrow?" ✓
- d) "Any questions?"

- a) "No, that is wrong."
- b) "I already told you."
- c) "Read the leaflet."
- d) "Thank you, let me explain that part again in a clearer way." ✓

**Q6.** Which is the plainest way to say "twice daily" to a customer?
- a) "Bid"
- b) "Morning and evening" ✓
- c) "Every twelve hours, orally"
- d) "Two doses per diem"

- a) Calm, open posture, brief eye contact and a warm voice ✓
- b) Crossed arms and a quick tone to save time

**Q9.** Which phone behaviour is correct?
- a) Put the caller on hold without telling them

**Q10.** Which WhatsApp practice is correct?
- a) Use the approved Lotus account and templates, and confirm product, quantity and price in EGP ✓

**Q11.** A customer asks on WhatsApp whether a medicine can be combined with others for a toddler. What is best?
- a) Answer yes quickly
- b) Ignore the question
- c) Send a long audio explanation
- d) Politely invite the customer to call or visit so that the pharmacist can get the full details ✓

**Q12.** An older customer comes with a relative who answers every question. What is the best approach?
- a) Talk only to the relative
- b) Speak louder and faster
- c) Greet and face the older customer, speak slowly, write a large schedule and use teach-back ✓

**Q13.** A woman asks for a medicine and you do not know whether she is pregnant or breastfeeding. What should you do?
- a) Assume she is not
- b) Ask kindly and privately, check a reliable reference and refer to the doctor when in doubt ✓

**Q14.** A caregiver collects medicines for a patient. Which is correct?
- a) Hand over the bag without a word
- b) Counsel only the patient who is not present

**Q15.** A customer asks for a prescription-only antibiotic "because it worked last time". Which reply follows the four-part no?
- a) "I understand you want to feel better fast. It needs a prescription because the wrong choice can cause harm. I can suggest something for your symptoms and tell you when to see a doctor." ✓
