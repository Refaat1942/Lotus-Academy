## Course description

Clinical knowledge helps a customer only when it is communicated well. In a busy Lotus branch, a pharmacist may serve dozens of customers in an hour, answer the phone, reply to WhatsApp orders and coordinate with colleagues at the same time. This course gives pharmacists and pharmacy staff practical communication tools that fit that reality: listening properly in a few seconds, explaining medicines in plain Arabic, confirming understanding, and staying calm when a conversation becomes difficult.

The course covers six areas: active listening and empathy, counselling and the teach-back method, non-verbal, telephone and WhatsApp communication, communicating with special groups, difficult conversations and saying no professionally, and finally teamwork, handover and confidentiality. Every lesson includes model dialogues in English and Arabic, checklists, common mistakes and scenarios taken from daily work in Egyptian pharmacies.

Each lesson takes about 85 minutes and ends with a 10-question quiz. A 20-question final exam covers the whole course. The course teaches communication behaviour only; clinical decisions, legal requirements and dispensing rules always follow the current rules of the Egyptian Drug Authority / Ministry of Health and the Lotus SOP.

## Learning outcomes

1. Apply active listening and empathy to understand the real need behind a customer's request
2. Explain dose, timing and storage in plain Arabic and confirm understanding with the teach-back method
3. Use tone, body language, telephone and WhatsApp communication in a professional and private way
4. Adapt communication for older adults, children and parents, pregnant and breastfeeding women, people with low health literacy, sensory difficulties and caregivers
5. Say no politely and de-escalate angry customers while keeping safety and boundaries
6. Hand over information accurately between shifts and protect patient confidentiality at the counter
7. Give and receive feedback in a way that strengthens the pharmacy team
